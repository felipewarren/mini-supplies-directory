Dollhouse and miniature shops and museums in Connecticut

Last checked: November 20, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or museum's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Connecticut has a full dollhouse shop in Greenwich, a dollhouse builder's workshop in Middlebury, and four museums with dollhouses on view: a 1962 Victorian dollhouse in New London, a Victorian dolls' house in New Haven, a large toy and dollhouse collection in Wilton, and a doll house in a historic house in Granby. We found no Connecticut miniature show with a posted 2027 date and no club with its own web page, so see the tips below for nearby shows. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-20).

Whimsies Dollhouse Shop — Greenwich

A dollhouse shop that sells heirloom-quality dollhouses at every stage: custom-designed, ready-built or as do-it-yourself kits. It also carries kits, trim, paint, wallpaper, shingles and other dollhouse parts, collectible miniature furniture, miniature accessories and porcelain dolls. Address: 39 Lewis Street, Greenwich, CT 06830 (side entrance under the black awning, between Greenwich Avenue and Mason Street). Phone: 203-629-8024. Holiday hours through Christmas: Sunday noon to 4 p.m., Monday closed, Tuesday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., Wednesday 10:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., Thursday and Friday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Closed Thanksgiving Day, November 26, through Saturday, November 28, and on Christmas Day; it closes at 3:30 p.m. on Christmas Eve. The website shows no online shop and says nothing about shipping.

Source: whimsiesdollhouseshop.com, contact page · checked 2026-11-20

Dollhouse Mansions — Middlebury

A family dollhouse builder in business since 1988. It sells dollhouse kits from makers such as Real Good Toys, assembled houses and finished houses, and will build and finish a kit you already own. Its custom-finished houses are 1-inch scale with hard-wired electrical, and colonials, Victorians, farmhouses, lighthouses and firehouses are among the styles it builds. Finished dollhouses are on display at its workshop in Middlebury, CT 06762. Phone (cell): 203-768-3816. The website gives no street address or opening hours, so call before you visit.

Source: dollhousemansions.com · checked 2026-11-20

Museums

Lyman Allyn Art Museum — New London

An art museum with a family gallery, Playthings of the Past, that shows toys and dolls from its collection to visitors aged 3 to 10. Its centrepiece is the American Victorian Dollhouse, built for the museum in 1962 by Harold E. Hawthorne. It has three floors with bedrooms, a dining room, a nursery, a sewing room and two kitchens, filled with miniatures from the Baratz Collection that range from the Victorian era to the early 20th century. Address: 625 Williams Street, New London, CT 06320. Phone: 860-443-2545. Hours: Tuesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Sunday 1 p.m. to 5 p.m., closed Monday; last entry at 4 p.m. Work on a new park around the museum is under way, but the museum keeps its normal hours.

Source: Playthings of the Past, visit page · checked 2026-11-20

New Haven Museum — New Haven

The city's history museum shows the Levy Dolls' House, a Victorian house handmade in the late 1800s, in the lower half of its rotunda; the museum's summer 2026 gallery guide lists it among its "old favorites". The house holds 482 objects, including a music-box piano, a hand-crank wall telephone and a cast-iron stove, and it was donated to the museum in 1994. Address: 114 Whitney Avenue, New Haven, CT 06510. Phone: 203-562-4183. Its hours page lists Wednesday to Friday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday noon to 5 p.m.; it is closed Thanksgiving weekend, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

Source: summer 2026 gallery guide (PDF), museum page on the Levy Dolls' House, hours page · checked 2026-11-21

Wilton Historical Society — Wilton

A local history museum whose permanent exhibition of toys, dolls and dollhouses is one of the largest in the state. It covers 150 years of childhood objects, with dolls, dollhouses, toys, Raggedy Ann dolls, iron and tin vehicles, banks and games. Address: 224 Danbury Road, Wilton, CT 06897. Phone: 203-762-7257. Usual hours are Tuesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The museum is closed until November 27 while it sets up its Great Trains Holiday Show; during the show (late November to January) it is open Wednesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. and Sunday noon to 4 p.m. Call to check that the toy gallery is open during the train show.

Source: Toys and Dolls, visit page · checked 2026-11-20

Salmon Brook Historical Society — Granby

A group of historic buildings in Granby. Upstairs in the Rowe House, a room of Victorian toys includes a doll house, which the society calls "exquisite", along with antique dolls. Address: 208 Salmon Brook Street, Granby, CT 06035. Phone: 860-653-9713. The museum's season has ended for the year; tours are available by appointment over the winter, and it reopens in the spring.

Source: museum buildings, visit page, events page · checked 2026-11-20
