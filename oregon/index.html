Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and clubs in Oregon

Last checked: November 14, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, show's or club's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Oregon is a natural next stop after Washington for British Columbia builders: Portland hosts a yearly miniature show, and the state has a hobby shop with a dollhouse section, a Portland kit maker and an active makers' club in Eugene. This page lists what we could confirm. For online US shops, see the USA page; for Seattle and the rest of Washington, see the Washington page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-14).

RK Hobbies & Miniatures (ARK Miniature Enterprises) — La Pine

A hobby store south of Bend that sells radio-control vehicles, trains and model kits alongside a dollhouse section: dollhouse furniture and kits, wallpaper, flooring, fabrics and accessories. Address: 51470 US Hwy 97, Suite 2, La Pine, OR 97739. Its site lists the store as open Tuesday to Saturday, closing at 6 p.m. Phone: 541-280-7206. Email: arkminiatures@bendbroadband.com. The site doesn't mention online ordering or shipping, so call ahead.

Source: arkminiatures.net, Visit Us page · checked 2026-11-14

Division Street Miniatures — Portland

A Portland miniature kit company that designs and produces laser-cut dollhouse, furniture and architectural kits in its own workshop. The online catalogue includes wainscoting and wall panels, fireplaces, windows, trim and furniture kits, mostly in 1:12 with some pieces also in 1:24. Prices are in US dollars, and shipping is calculated at checkout; its shipping terms for Canada aren't stated on the pages we checked. Email: info@divisionstminiatures.com.

Source: divisionstminiatures.com · checked 2026-11-14

Shows

Portland Miniature Show (PNW Miniature Shows)

A yearly dollhouse miniature show with dealers, workshops, exhibits and "Mini Acres", a section for buying and selling whole dollhouses. The organizers say the next Portland show is in October 2027; exact dates, venue and tickets for 2027 aren't posted yet. The 2026 show ran October 16–18, 2026, at the Sheraton Portland Airport Hotel (8235 NE Airport Way), with a Friday-evening preview and free parking. The same organizers run the Seattle show, next held March 5–7, 2027 (see the Washington page).

Source: pnwminiatureshows.com, Portland show page · checked 2026-11-14

Clubs

Eugene Miniatures Club — Eugene-Springfield

A hands-on "let's make it" club for miniature makers. Members build roomboxes, scale furniture and accessories, take trips to shows, and look after a model house. Meetings are held in person on the first Monday of the month, 7 to 9 p.m., and on Zoom on Thursdays, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m.; contact the club through its website for the location or link. Its latest post (November 6, 2026) describes its plans for 2027.

Source: eugeneminiatures.org · checked 2026-11-14
