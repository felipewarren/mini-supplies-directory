Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut: shops and suppliers

Last checked: November 7, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

We couldn't confirm a dedicated 1:12 dollhouse shop in Yukon, the Northwest Territories or Nunavut. What we could confirm is a Whitehorse craft shop that sells DIY miniature house kits (plus other craft kits and art supplies), the Yukon Art Society's art-supply shop in downtown Whitehorse, and a Yellowknife hobby shop with acrylic paint, polymer clay and related art and craft goods. We didn't find a Nunavut shop whose own website confirmed miniature or scratch-building supplies, and we didn't find a territorial miniature show or club we could confirm from its own source. For a full 1:12 furniture and building-supply range, compare the online shops on the Canada page; Alberta makers and a Calgary hobby store that lists Canada-wide shipping are on the Alberta page, and dedicated shops in Vancouver are on the British Columbia page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Yukon

The Mini Makerspace — Whitehorse

A craft shop in Horwood’s Mall at 1116 Front Street, Unit 112, Whitehorse, YT Y1A 1A3. It isn’t a dedicated 1:12 dollhouse store, but its online catalog includes DIY miniature house kits. Examples we opened: DIY Miniature House Kit: Garage Workshop; DIY Miniature House Kit: Christmas Fireplace (ages 14+, 73 pieces, about 1 hour to assemble, assembled size 4.9 × 3.54 × 7.16 inches); and DIY Miniature House Kit: Fairytale Tree House (ages 14+, 275 pieces, assembled size 8.3 × 7.2 × 8.9 inches; needs 2 AAA batteries, not included). Other shop categories include 3D wooden puzzle décor kits, clay kits, watercolour, drawing and sketching, pencils and pens, cross stitch and pipe-cleaner kits. These scene kits list assembled sizes rather than a 1:12 scale, so check the scale before mixing them with dollhouse furniture. Posted hours are Tuesday–Saturday 10:30 am–5:30 pm; Monday and Sunday are closed but listed as available for bookings. Phone: (867) 333-3883. Email: TheMiniMakerspace@gmail.com. The site has an online shop; shipping terms were not stated on the pages we checked.

Source: theminimakerspace.com (location and hours), Garage Workshop kit, Christmas Fireplace kit, Fairytale Tree House kit · checked 2026-11-07

Arts Underground — Whitehorse

A community arts centre run by the Yukon Art Society, in the Hougen Centre at 15-305 Main Street, Whitehorse, YT Y1A 2B4. It isn’t a miniature or dollhouse store. Besides galleries, workshops and a studio, it sells art supplies and describes that shop as one of the widest ranges of professional-grade art supplies available in the Yukon, in store or online. The online art-supplies catalog we checked included watercolour travel kits (Lukas and Cotman), Micron and Pigma pens, palettes, Speedy-Carve blocks and lino-cutting tools — useful for paints, drawing and finishing work. Posted hours are Tuesday–Friday 10 am–5 pm, Saturday 11 am–5 pm, closed Sunday and Monday and on Yukon statutory holidays. Phone: (867) 667-4080. Email: reception@artsunderground.ca.

Source: about, contact and hours, art supplies shop · checked 2026-11-07

Northwest Territories

Juniper Hobbies — Yellowknife

A hobby shop at 4907 47th Street, Yellowknife, NT X1A 1L7, inside Juniper Health. It isn’t a dollhouse supplier. Its online catalog is grouped into art supplies (canvas, art tools, acrylic paint, handmade water paint, modeling clay, markers and pencils), crafts (including paint-by-number, diamond painting, clay pottery and embroidery kits), LEGO, puzzles, board and card games, toys and books. When we checked, the acrylic-paint collection listed Art Noise acrylic paint by Tri-Art at $8.00, and the modeling-clay collection listed a DAS Smart polymer clay 57 g block at $5.00 and a DAS Smart primary set (12 × 28 g) at $38.00. Hours were not stated on the pages we checked. Phone: (867) 873-3669. Email: info@juniperhealthclinic.com. Shipping terms were not stated on the homepage or contact page.

Nunavut

We couldn’t confirm a shop in Nunavut whose own website showed dollhouse miniatures or scratch-building supplies. If you know one, please send a correction. For furniture, kits and building supplies by mail, compare the online shops on the Canada page.

Tips

  • We couldn’t confirm a dedicated 1:12 dollhouse shop, club or miniature show in Yukon, the Northwest Territories or Nunavut. The Free Miniaturist Directory’s Canada page has headings for the ten provinces but none for Yukon, the Northwest Territories or Nunavut.
  • Alberta makers such as MiniMinutia (Edmonton) and Fantastic Minis sell 1:12 supplies online, and PM Hobbycraft in Calgary lists Canada-wide shipping (see the Alberta page). The Miniature Enthusiasts of Edmonton club also meets online, so you can join from anywhere in Canada.
  • For a larger walk-in 1:12 range, the dedicated shops we know of are farther south, including M for Miniatures and Ross’ Miniature Treasure House in the Vancouver area (see the British Columbia page).
  • DIY miniature house kits and hobby-shop figures often use a different scale than a 1:12 dollhouse. Check the scale before you buy.
