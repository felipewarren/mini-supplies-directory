Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and museums in Ohio

Last checked: November 18, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, show's, club's or museum's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Ohio has one small dollhouse shop you can visit near East Liverpool, two online shops run from the Columbus and Cleveland areas, a well-established club show near Cincinnati, and three museums with dollhouses. It also has three active miniature clubs, including one of the oldest in the world. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-18).

Three Sisters Miniatures — East Liverpool

A family-run dollhouse shop in the Calcutta area. It sells ready-made dollhouses and kits, flooring, wallpaper, holiday decor, building supplies (doors, windows, stairs, roofing), furniture, accessories, and lights and wiring kits, and it offers a wiring service. It also sells its own handmade items, such as wooden furniture, crocheted rugs and linens, curtains and polymer clay pieces, and it will build a dollhouse to order. Address: 49430 Berkshire Rd., East Liverpool, OH 43920. Posted hours: Tuesday 6 p.m. to 9 p.m. and Saturday 10 a.m. to noon, and by appointment. The shop says it doesn't always open at the posted hours, so call 330-843-7013 before a long drive.

Source: threesistersminiatures.weebly.com · checked 2026-11-18

It's a Miniatures Life — Lewis Center (online)

An online shop run by two sisters who have been dollhouse miniature enthusiasts for more than 30 years. It sells manufactured, handmade and artisan-made 1:12 accessories, sorted by room and theme (kitchen, baking, food, garden, hardware and construction, holiday, estate pieces and more), and takes custom orders. There are no on-site sales. Orders outside the continental US are possible, but you need to ask for a shipping quote first; the site does not name Canada. Phone: 614-404-8874.

Source: itsaminiatureslife.com, contact page, shipping and returns · checked 2026-11-18

The Miniature Cellar — Chesterland (online)

A shop for collectors of fine miniatures, with the owner's own work and pieces by many artisans, plus estate miniatures and unusual items such as nautical brass. It no longer has a store you can visit; it sells on its website and at shows. Important for Canadians: its shipping page says it sells only within the United States. Use the contact form on its website for questions.

Source: miniaturecellar.com, shipping and returns · checked 2026-11-18

Shows

Miniature Society of Cincinnati Show & Sale — Mason

The yearly two-day show and sale of the Miniature Society of Cincinnati (see Clubs below). Next dates: Friday, May 21, 2027, 4 p.m. to 7 p.m., and Saturday, May 22, 2027, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Location: Heritage Presbyterian Church, 6546 S. Mason-Montgomery Road, Mason, OH. Dealers come from across North America, and club members show their room boxes and dollhouses in the exhibit rooms. There are door prizes, a sales table just for kids 12 and under, and a table of free used miniature magazines. Admission is cash only; keep your wristband and you can come back on the second day without paying again. Free parking; the building is accessible. No strollers, backpacks, large bags or wheeled carts.

Source: cincy-miniatures.org show page, 2026 show announcement (address) · checked 2026-11-18

Museums

Making Miniatures, Children's Museum Cleveland — Cleveland

A dollhouse exhibit in colourful rooms at the top of the museum: four galleries of handmade dollhouse displays in different styles and themes, with homes and dolls from different periods and cultures, including a replica of the museum's own home, the historic Stager-Beckwith mansion. Children can hunt for items with flashlights and play in a hands-on dollhouse space. Address: 3813 Euclid Avenue, Cleveland, OH. Phone: 216-791-7114. The exhibit page says the museum is closed on Thursdays; check its hours page before you go.

Source: cmcleveland.org exhibit page · checked 2026-11-18

Children's Toy and Doll Museum — Marietta

Nine rooms of antique and vintage toys and dolls in an Eastlake/Queen Anne-style house, with highlights such as the Old Woman in the Shoe dollhouse and a model of Wendelken's General Store in Marietta. The collection began with toys, games, dolls and dollhouses gathered by a local women's club in 1976. Open from May to October. Address: 206 Gilman Ave., Marietta, OH 45750. Phone: 740-373-5900 (also for group tours).

Source: toyanddoll.org, about page · checked 2026-11-18

Georgian Museum — Lancaster, Ohio

An 1832 house museum whose guided tour includes dollhouses and one-of-a-kind antique miniatures, along with restored period rooms. (This is Lancaster, Ohio, not the Lancaster, Pennsylvania, of the Lancaster show.) The 2026 season ends December 6, 2026: open Wednesday to Saturday, with guided tours starting at noon, 1, 2 and 3 p.m.; a tour takes about an hour. Only the first floor is wheelchair accessible. Address: 105 East Wheeling Street, Lancaster, OH 43130. Phone: 740-654-9923. The 2027 season dates were not posted yet.

Source: thegeorgianmuseum.org · checked 2026-11-18

Clubs

Miniature Society of Cincinnati — Cincinnati

A hands-on N.A.M.E. club founded in 1975, with around 50 members, which runs the Mason show above. It meets on the second Wednesday of each month at 7 p.m. at Kenwood Fellowship Church, 7205 Kenwood Road, Cincinnati, OH 45236 (a few exceptions are on its meetings page). Guests 16 and older are welcome at regular meetings; to join, attend one meeting first and also join N.A.M.E.

Source: cincy-miniatures.org about page, meetings page · checked 2026-11-18

Columbus Miniature Society — Grove City

A N.A.M.E. club that meets on the third Saturday of the month at St John's Evangelical Lutheran Church, 3220 Columbus St., Grove City, OH 43123: socializing from 12:30 p.m., a short business meeting at 1 p.m., then a hands-on project from 1:30 to about 3:30 p.m. All skill levels are welcome; bring a basic tool box. Planned 2027 projects include quarter-scale market stalls (January 16) and miniature houses (February 20).

Source: columbusminiaturesociety.org, meetings page · checked 2026-11-18

Cleveland Miniaturia Society — Fairview Park

Founded on April 18, 1971, and chartered by N.A.M.E. (club C-0066), it calls itself the second-oldest scale miniature club in the world. It meets on the second Wednesday of the month at 7 p.m. at the Fairview Park Library, 21255 Lorain Rd., Fairview Park, OH 44126; there is no meeting in January, and the August and December meetings are potlucks at 6 p.m. Each meeting has a project taught by a member, in 1", 1/2" and 1/4" scale. Membership is for adults 18 and older. The club no longer runs its October show.

Source: clevelandminiaturiasociety.org, calendar · checked 2026-11-18

Tips

  • Call Three Sisters Miniatures before you drive there; its hours are short and not guaranteed.
  • The Georgian Museum closes for the season on December 6, 2026, and the Children's Toy and Doll Museum is only open from May to October.
  • The Mason show is a Friday evening and a Saturday. If you also want the N.A.M.E. National Convention in Louisville, Kentucky (August 12 to 15, 2027), see our events page.
  • Ordering from the US? Ask for the shipping cost to Canada before you pay, and remember that duty and taxes may apply. Read how to avoid scams first.
