Dollhouse and miniature shops, museums, shows and clubs in Arizona

Last checked: November 24, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, museum's, show's or club's own website (for clubs, the club list of the National Association of Miniature Enthusiasts, NAME). Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Arizona has two good stops for miniaturists. In Tucson, the Mini Time Machine Museum of Miniatures shows more than 500 handcrafted scenes and has a museum store with fine-scale and artisan miniatures. It also holds a big summer sale of donated miniatures, and the local club's show each October raises money for it. In the Phoenix area, Phoenix Art Museum has 20 of Narcissa Thorne's famous miniature rooms, Glendale has a walk-in miniature shop, and the Small World Miniature Club holds its yearly show in Glendale on March 13 and 14, 2027. We also list five NAME clubs. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-24).

Auntie Em's Miniatures — Glendale

A dollhouse and miniature shop in Glendale, in the Phoenix area. Its website lists dollhouses, furniture, lighting, accessories and kits, mostly in 1:12 scale. The online store on the website only shows sample items with no prices, so call or email the shop to ask what is in stock before you make the trip. Address: 5625 W. Glendale Avenue, Glendale, AZ 85301. Phone: 623-435-0783. Email: auntieem@gmail.com. Hours: Monday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; closed Sunday. NAME's southwestern region also lists it among the miniature shops to visit in the region.

Source: auntie-emsminiatures.com, NAME Region N-1 "Places to Go" page · checked 2026-11-24

Museum Store at the Mini Time Machine Museum of Miniatures — Tucson

The museum's store sells fine-scale and artisan miniatures, work by local artists, books, toys and souvenirs. Museum members get 10% off eligible purchases, and all proceeds support the museum. Address: 4455 E. Camp Lowell Drive, Tucson, AZ 85712. Phone: 520-881-0606. The museum is open Tuesday to Sunday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. The website doesn't say whether you can visit the store without a museum ticket, so ask at the front desk.

Source: plan your visit page · checked 2026-11-24

Shows and sales

Small World Miniature Club Show & Sale — Glendale

The yearly dollhouse miniature show and sale of the Small World Miniature Club of Phoenix, a NAME club, with exhibits, sales, door prizes and raffles, a silent auction on both days and a free make-and-take project for children over 4. Next show: Saturday, March 13, and Sunday, March 14, 2027, at a new location: Glendale Community College, 6000 W. Olive Avenue, Glendale, AZ 85302. The announcement gives the hours as Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Sunday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. (the club's event calendar shows an 11 a.m. start for Saturday, so confirm before you go). Admission is paid, with a lower price for children.

Source: 2027 show page, dealers page · checked 2026-11-24

Tucson Miniature Society Show & Sale — Tucson

The Tucson Miniature Society's yearly show and sale, held each October, with dealers from around the country, displays, door prizes, raffles and a kids' sales table and projects. Since 2016 the proceeds have gone to the Mini Time Machine Museum of Miniatures. The 2026 show, the club's 50th, was on October 24 and 25 (Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.) at the Tucson Woman's Club, 6245 E. Bellevue Street, Tucson. One paid admission was good for both days and included a free ticket to the Mini Time Machine. The 2027 dates are not posted yet; check the club's website.

Source: show and sale page, Tucson Miniature Society home page · checked 2026-11-24

Tiny Treasures Sale at the Mini Time Machine — Tucson

The museum's yearly sale of thousands of donated miniatures: dollhouses, furniture, room boxes, accessories, building supplies and one-of-a-kind finds, at thrift prices. Entry to the sale is free (museum admission is separate), payment is cash or cheque only, and you enter through the museum patio. Museum members can shop a day early. The 2026 sale was on Saturday, August 1 (9 a.m. to 4 p.m.), after a members' preview on Friday, July 31; the 2025 sale was in late July. The 2027 date is not announced yet; watch the museum's calendar.

Source: 2026 Tiny Treasures Sale page, 2025 sale page · checked 2026-11-24

Museums

The Mini Time Machine Museum of Miniatures — Tucson

A museum of miniature art with more than 500 handcrafted scenes, from historic interiors and architectural models to fantasy worlds, built on the collection of its founders, Pat and Walter Arnell, who were active in NAME and the International Guild of Miniature Artisans (IGMA). Exhibits now on view include The Need to Keep, miniature works by Amanda Kelly (until June 20, 2027), and Ara Bentley's Addams Family Mini Mansion and Beetlejuice dollhouse (until November 5, 2028). For the holidays, the museum decorates a number of its miniatures for Holiday Traditions in Miniature (until January 3, 2027). It also runs artist-led workshops for adults, such as building a 1:12 scale 17th-century kitchen room box with Ara Bentley. Address: 4455 E. Camp Lowell Drive, Tucson, AZ 85712. Phone: 520-881-0606. Hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. (last admission 3:30 p.m.); closed Mondays, July 4, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day, and it closes at noon on New Year's Eve. Admission is paid; members get in free, and children under 12 must come with an adult. Groups of 8 or more should book at least two weeks ahead.

Source: theminitimemachine.org, plan your visit page, mission and history page, current exhibitions, adult workshops page · checked 2026-11-24

Thorne Miniature Rooms at Phoenix Art Museum — Phoenix

Twenty of the original 30 Thorne Miniature Rooms, period rooms from the United States and Europe built at 1:12 scale under Narcissa Niblack Thorne in the 1930s and shown at the 1933 Chicago world's fair. Thorne gave them to the museum in 1962, and they have been on view ever since, now in the museum's Ballinger Wing. More Thorne rooms are at the Art Institute of Chicago (see our Illinois page). Address: 1625 North Central Avenue, Phoenix, AZ 85004. Phone: 602-257-1880. Hours: Wednesday and Friday, 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.; Thursday and Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Sunday, noon to 5 p.m.; closed Monday, Tuesday, New Year's Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas. Admission is paid, but Wednesdays from 3 p.m. to 8 p.m. are pay-what-you-wish.

Source: Thorne Miniature Rooms page, visit page · checked 2026-11-24

Clubs

These five clubs are on NAME's club list as accepting new members. The list gives a contact person for each club.

Small World Miniature Club — Glendale

A friendly club for miniaturists in the Phoenix area, from beginners to experienced makers, that runs the Glendale show. It meets on the second Saturday of the month, 9:30 a.m. to 1 p.m., in the Parish Hall of St. Andrews Episcopal Church, 6300 W. Camelback Road, Glendale, AZ 85301 (enter the parking lot from 63rd Avenue). Guests and visitors are welcome.

Source: phoenixminiatures.com, NAME club list (linked from miniatures.org) · checked 2026-11-24

Tucson Miniature Society — Tucson

A club founded 50 years ago whose members work in many scales and subjects, with group projects, show-and-tell, classes and demonstrations. It meets on the first Saturday of the month at 10:30 a.m. (meet and greet from 10 a.m.), or on the second Saturday when the first falls on a holiday weekend. NAME's list gives the meeting place as the Mini Time Machine Museum of Miniatures; the club asks you to check its Facebook page or email it for meeting details.

Source: Tucson Miniature Society home page, NAME club list · checked 2026-11-24

Desert Mini-Makers — Mesa

A NAME club in the east Phoenix area. It meets on the first Saturday of the month, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., in the Think Room of the Mesa Public Library, 64 E. First Street, Mesa, AZ 85201.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-24

More or Less (A Smaller Scales Club) — Mesa

A NAME club for the smaller scales. It meets on the first Saturday of the month, 9 a.m. to noon, in the Think Spot of the Mesa Public Library, 64 E. First Street, Mesa, AZ 85201.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-24

Saguaro Min. Guild of Yuma — Yuma

A NAME club in southwestern Arizona that meets in members' homes, usually on a Saturday; times vary.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-24

Tips

  • Spending part of the winter in Arizona? Plan around the Glendale show (March 13 and 14, 2027), and visit the Thorne rooms in Phoenix and Auntie Em's in Glendale on the same trip.
  • In Tucson, a ticket to the Tucson Miniature Society's October show has included free admission to the Mini Time Machine, so you can see both on one weekend.
