Dollhouse and miniature shops, museums, shows and clubs in Missouri

Last checked: November 25, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, museum's or show's own website (for clubs, the local clubs list of the Miniature Museum of Greater St. Louis). Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Missouri has two museums worth a trip for miniaturists. In Kansas City, the National Museum of Toys and Miniatures gives its whole first floor to artist-made fine-scale miniatures. In St. Louis, the volunteer-run Miniature Museum of Greater St. Louis fills two floors with room boxes and dollhouses and has a large gift shop for hobbyists. Near St. Louis, O'Fallon has a walk-in dollhouse shop, and the St. Louis Dollhouse and Miniature Show was held in Bridgeton in October 2026 (2027 dates not posted yet). We also list the museum's own sales and three local clubs. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-25).

Suzanne's Dollhouses and Miniatures — O'Fallon

A brick-and-mortar dollhouse and miniature shop in O'Fallon, outside St. Louis, run by a miniaturist with more than 45 years in the hobby. The owner says most items in the shop were made by her or come from estates she bought, so stock is often one of a kind; her own pieces are mostly in 1:12 (one inch) scale. If you need a particular item, contact her and she will try to find it. Address: 201 O'Fallon Plaza, O'Fallon, MO 63366. Phone: 314-495-9714. Email: suzanne@suzannesdollhouses.com. Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday. The shop was also a dealer at the 2026 St. Louis Dollhouse and Miniature Show, and the Miniature Museum of Greater St. Louis lists it among local shops.

Source: suzannesdollhouses.com, contact and hours page · checked 2026-11-25

Gift shop at the Miniature Museum of Greater St. Louis — St. Louis

The museum's large gift shop sells miniatures it buys at shows or receives from donors, so the stock changes often and many items are one of a kind. Its list includes dollhouses, furniture and furniture kits, lighting, wallpaper, flooring, windows, doors, stairs and trim, kitchen items, food, plants, rugs, and Christmas and Halloween pieces. It also sells light panels and LEDs. Museum members get 10% off. Address: 4746 Gravois Avenue, St. Louis, MO 63116. Phone: 314-832-7790. Gift shop hours: Friday and Saturday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Sunday, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. The website doesn't say whether you can shop without a museum ticket, so ask at the desk.

Source: gift shop page · checked 2026-11-25

Shows and sales

St. Louis Dollhouse and Miniature Show — Bridgeton

A dollhouse and miniature show run by T & D Miniatures, with artists and dealers from across the country and classes on the Friday. The 2026 show was on October 2 and 3 (Friday 4 p.m. to 8 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.) at Machinist Hall, 12365 St. Charles Rock Road, Bridgeton, MO, near St. Louis. Tickets were good for both days and sold at the door, cash only. The 2027 dates are not posted yet; check the organizer's website. Organizer: 314-277-2601, tdminiatures@gmail.com.

Source: St. Louis show page, miniatureshows.com · checked 2026-11-25

Miniature sales at the Miniature Museum of Greater St. Louis — St. Louis

The museum holds two sales a year. In April, its Miniature Sellers Garage Sale fills the museum classroom and the neighbouring Fireman's Hall on Delor Street with hobbyists selling their extra miniatures. In August, a Museum Only Mini Sale offers furniture, supplies, dollhouses, room boxes, landscaping and accessories in all scales; the 2026 sale was on Saturday, August 22, from 9 a.m. to noon. The 2027 dates are not posted yet; the museum announces them on its website and on Facebook and Instagram. The museum also runs miniature workshops in its classroom, which has its own entrance on Delor Street.

Source: sales page, about page, classes, shows, shops and clubs page · checked 2026-11-25

Museums

The National Museum of Toys and Miniatures — Kansas City

A museum with two floors: artist-made fine-scale miniatures on the first floor and historic and modern toys, including dollhouses, on the second. The fine-scale miniatures, typically at 1:12 scale, range from miniature rooms and buildings to tiny musical instruments, paintings, tools and food. Since 1982 the museum has gathered more than 100,000 objects. Exhibitions now on view include Native Arts in Miniature, work by Native American artists working in miniature (until January 25, 2027), and an Art of the Imagination case featuring Coti Meier (until April 19, 2027). Plan at least 90 minutes. The museum store sells handmade items and gifts and keeps the museum's hours. Address: 5235 Oak Street, Kansas City, MO 64112, on the University of Missouri–Kansas City campus (parking is free, entrance off 52nd Street). Phone: 816-235-8000. Hours: Wednesday to Monday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Tuesday and on MLK Day, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Admission is paid, but free on the second Sunday of every month; the museum suggests booking tickets online because space is limited.

Source: toyandminiaturemuseum.org, plan your visit page, FAQ, current exhibitions, museum store page · checked 2026-11-25

Miniature Museum of Greater St. Louis — St. Louis

A volunteer-run museum with 6,200 square feet of exhibits on two floors: room boxes, dollhouses and displays in scales from 1:6 down to micro scale, from historic kitchens and rustic cabins to mansions, haunted houses, gardens and shops. Started in 1989, it opened in its building in the Bevo Mill neighbourhood of south St. Louis in 2001. It also has a hobby library and the gift shop listed above. Address: 4746 Gravois Avenue, St. Louis, MO 63116. Phone: 314-832-7790. Hours: Friday and Saturday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Sunday, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Monday to Thursday and on New Year's Day, Easter, Mother's Day, Father's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve. In snow, ice or extreme weather, call before you go. Only the first floor is wheelchair accessible, and children under 16 must come with an adult. Admission is paid (members and active-duty service members free; children under 4 free); groups of 15 or more get a discount. Free parking behind the building on Delor Street, in front on Gravois Avenue and in the Bevo Mill lot across the street.

Source: miniaturemuseum.org, hours and admission page, about page · checked 2026-11-25

Tips

  • In the St. Louis area, go on a Friday or Saturday: the Miniature Museum and its gift shop are open, and so is Suzanne's in O'Fallon, west of the city.
  • In Kansas City, the National Museum of Toys and Miniatures is free on the second Sunday of the month, and it is closed on Tuesdays.
