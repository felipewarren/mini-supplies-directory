Dollhouse and miniature shops, museums, shows and clubs in Indiana

Last checked: November 23, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, museum's, show's or club's own website (for clubs, the club list of the National Association of Miniature Enthusiasts, NAME). Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Central Indiana is a good stop for miniaturists. Carmel, just north of Indianapolis, has the Museum of Miniature Houses, one of the few museums in the US devoted to fine scale miniatures, with a museum shop you can visit without paying admission. It also holds a big Attic Sale of miniatures each spring. In Indianapolis, the Children's Museum has a permanent miniatures gallery, and a family bakery sells dollhouse miniatures in its gift shop. Each September a dollhouse and miniature show is held in Westfield. We also list two Indiana shops that sell online and four NAME clubs. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-23).

Museum Shop at the Museum of Miniature Houses — Carmel

The museum's shop stocks furniture, rugs, wallpaper, lamps, dolls, food, plants and more, plus building materials, hardware and many furniture kits: beginner kits, artisan pieces for experts and souvenirs. It also sells cordless miniature lamps designed by Carl R. Sahlberg (CR2S) that you turn on and off by tapping the base with a small wand. Dollhouses, house kits and room boxes for sale are kept off-site; call 317-575-9466 to ask about them. You don't pay admission to visit the shop. Address: 111 East Main Street, Carmel, IN 46032. Hours: Wednesday to Saturday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Sunday, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Monday, Tuesday and some holidays, including Thanksgiving. The shop does not sell online; the shop page says the items shown are examples and may no longer be available.

Source: museum shop page, hours and admission page · checked 2026-11-23

Hart Bakery and Gifts — Indianapolis

A family-run bakery on the east side of Indianapolis with a gift shop that carries dollhouse miniatures, from chairs and dressers to chandeliers. The bakery says it is one of only four places in Indiana with such a large stock of dollhouse miniatures. Address: 7030 E. 10th Street, Indianapolis, IN 46219. Phone: 317-357-4706. Hours: Tuesday to Friday, 6 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.; Saturday, 6 a.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday. The miniatures page is not dated, so call to check what is in stock before a long drive.

Source: dollhouse miniatures page, contact page · checked 2026-11-23

All About Miniatures — Whiteland

An online shop with more than 12,000 dollhouse miniatures in 1:12, 1:24 and 1:48 scale: handmade trees, flowers and landscaping, furniture, kits, accessories, miniature food, building materials, wallpaper, lighting, hardware and tools. Orders ship from Whiteland, Indiana. When we visited from Canada, the site offered flat-rate shipping to Canada with import duties included and no fees on delivery. The website lists no shop to visit.

Source: allaboutminiatures.com, contact page · checked 2026-11-23

Jeepers Miniatures — Chesterton

A full-service dollhouse shop since 1992 that now sells online only. It carries dollhouses and kits in 1:12, 1:24, 1:48 and 1:144 scale, plans, building materials, lighting and wiring, wallpaper and flooring, furniture, dolls and accessories from many brands. Most orders ship the next business day by USPS (some by UPS). Canada is not on its current list of international destinations, so contact the shop before ordering from Canada. Phone: 812-597-4346. Email: info@jeepersminiatures.com.

Source: about page, shipping and returns page · checked 2026-11-23

Shows and sales

Attic Sale at the Museum of Miniature Houses — Carmel

The museum's yearly sale of miniatures, which draws buyers from across the country looking for projects, inspiration and bargains. All proceeds support the museum. Next sale: Saturday, April 24, 2027, at the Carmel Lions Club, next door to the museum on Main Street in Carmel. The announcement does not give the hours yet.

Source: 2027 Attic Sale announcement · checked 2026-11-23

Indianapolis Dollhouse and Miniature Show — Westfield

A dollhouse and miniature show with dealers and classes, held each September at the Wyndham Westfield (Cambria Hotel), 18592 Carousel Lane, Westfield, IN, north of Indianapolis. The 2026 show was on September 11 and 12 (Friday 4 p.m. to 8 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.), with tickets sold at the door, cash only, for both days. Dealers in 2026 included the Museum of Miniature Houses. The 2027 dates are not posted yet; check the show's website.

Source: miniatureshows.com Indianapolis show page · checked 2026-11-23

Museums

Museum of Miniature Houses — Carmel

Founded in 1993 by three Indiana artisans, this museum of fine art in miniature shows thousands of miniatures, including room boxes, miniature houses, antique dollhouses and single pieces, in seven exhibit rooms, and exhibits change often. Until June 2, 2027, Room 1 has Out of Many: America 250, miniatures from the collection that reflect American history. Every room has chairs and step stools. Visitors can also use a magnifying glass, free audio tours, treasure hunts for children and a dollhouse to play with, and guided tours can be booked in advance. The museum also runs miniature workshops (on December 5, 2026, a holiday mannequin class with miniaturist Nancye Claypool). Address: 111 East Main Street, Carmel, IN 46032. Phone: 317-575-9466. Hours: Wednesday to Saturday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Sunday, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Monday, Tuesday and some holidays. Admission is paid; members get in free, and children under 15 must come with an adult.

Source: museumofminiatures.org, about page, hours and admission page, exhibit page, workshop page · checked 2026-11-23

Mini Masterpieces at the Children's Museum of Indianapolis — Indianapolis

A permanent gallery on Level 3 of the Children's Museum with small, finely detailed re-creations of rooms and homes, including a miniature room by the well-known miniaturist Eugene Kupjack that shows the museum's first home as it looked in 1925. Children can try hands-on activities, design their own tiny world and play I Spy games. It is included with museum admission. Address: 3000 North Meridian Street, Indianapolis, IN 46208. Phone: 317-334-4000. Hours change with the season (see the museum's visit page), parking is free, and the museum will be closed from January 11 to 15, 2027, for maintenance.

Source: Mini Masterpieces page, story about the Kupjack room, visit page · checked 2026-11-23

Clubs

These four clubs are on NAME's club list as accepting new members. The list gives a contact person for each club.

Hoosier Mini-Mizers — Indianapolis

A NAME club based in Carmel. It meets on the third Saturday of the month at 1 p.m. at MCL, 2370 W. 86th Street, Indianapolis.

Source: NAME club list (linked from miniatures.org) · checked 2026-11-23

Madison Avenue Miniaturists — Franklin

A NAME club south of Indianapolis. It meets on the second and fourth Wednesdays of the month, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., at Turning Point Church, 3600 N. Morton Street, Franklin, IN 46131.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-23

Hoosier Mini Makers — Pendleton

A NAME club northeast of Indianapolis. It meets on the first Saturday of every month at noon (subject to change) at Pendleton Church of God, 636 E. Water Street, Pendleton, IN.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-23

Michiana Miniature Guild — Syracuse

A NAME club in northern Indiana that meets in members' homes at 9:30 a.m.: on the third Saturday of the month from February to September, and on the first Saturday in October and November. There are no meetings in December or January.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-23

Tips

  • Visiting Carmel? The Museum of Miniature Houses and its shop are open Wednesday to Sunday. NAME, the national miniature association, also has its headquarters, the NAME House and Museum, in Carmel at 130 N. Rangeline Road; its website gives no visiting hours, so call 317-571-8094 first.
  • Elsewhere in Indiana? NAME's club list also has a club that meets in members' homes in Muncie, and NAME's regional contacts page lists Indiana representatives who can point you to local clubs.
  • The 2027 NAME National Convention is in Louisville, Kentucky, a short drive south of Indiana. See our events page for it and other 2027 shows in the US and Canada.
  • Ordering from the US? Ask for the shipping cost to Canada before you pay, and remember that duty and taxes may apply. Read how to avoid scams first.
