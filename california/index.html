Dollhouse and miniature shops and shows in California

Last checked: November 11, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or show's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

California has only a few walk-in dollhouse shops left, but it hosts three big yearly miniature shows and many local clubs. This page lists the ones we could confirm. For online US shops that ship to California, see the USA page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Closed: Ms. Peggie's Place, San Diego's dollhouse and miniatures shop since 1980, announced it would close its Pacific Beach store on October 31, 2025, after a lease termination. The owner said she hoped to reopen elsewhere (SanDiegoVille).

My Doll's House — Torrance

A Los Angeles-area dollhouse miniature store with dollhouses, kits, collectibles, furnishings and lighting for both crafters and collectors. Its website is an information page, not an online store, so visit or call. Address: 1218 El Prado Ave, Ste 136, Torrance, CA 90501. Phone: 310-320-4828. Email: dollhouseminiaturestore@gmail.com.

San Diego Miniatures Show & Sale

A yearly show with exhibits, sales and classes, run by San Diego Miniature Crafters, a local volunteer non-profit. It's at the University of San Diego, Hahn University Center, 5998 Alcala Park Way, San Diego, CA 92110. Saturday 10 to 5 and Sunday 11 to 4. Admission is $10 for adults and $5 for children 8 to 12, and Sunday is free with a paid Saturday ticket. Tickets are sold at the door, and weekend parking is free. Adult workshops are on Friday, February 5, 2027.

Source: sdminiatureshow.com · checked 2026-11-11

Miniatures in the Wine Country — Lodi

The 15th annual show, with over 90 tables of miniatures plus dollhouses and room boxes. It's two shows in one place, and one admission gets you into both. Admission is $8 for 1 day or $10 for 2 days, youth 5 to 12 pay $3 for both days, and children under 5 get in free. It's at the Lodi Grape Festival grounds, 413 E. Lockeford Street, Lodi, CA 95240. On Saturday, Cabernet Hall is open 9 to 3 and Burgundy Hall 9 to 5; on Sunday, both are open 10 to 3.

Source: miniaturesinthewinecountry.com · checked 2026-11-11

Good Sam Showcase of Miniatures — San Jose

The 51st annual show, billed as the largest miniature show west of the Mississippi, raising money for the Auxiliary of Good Samaritan Hospital. It has sales tables with international dealers and IGMA artisans, exhibits, a white elephant sale and a sidewalk sale for used houses and kits. Saturday 10 to 5 and Sunday 11 to 4, with one-day workshops on Friday, October 8, 2027. Admission is $10 for adults (at the door only), $3 for youth 5 to 12, and free under 5. It's at the DoubleTree Hotel San Jose, 2050 Gateway Place, San Jose, CA 95110, and show parking is $5 a day.

Source: goodsamshowcase.org · checked 2026-11-11
