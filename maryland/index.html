Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and clubs in Maryland

Last checked: November 27, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or show's own website (for clubs, the club list of the National Association of Miniature Enthusiasts, NAME). Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

In Maryland, the main event for dollhouse fans is the Frederick Miniature Spectacular, a one-day show and sale in Walkersville, near Frederick, on September 25, 2027. In Manchester, on the Pennsylvania line, Virtual Dollhouse & Miniatures opens its small store by appointment and ships its stock of smaller-scale and metal miniatures abroad. We also list four NAME clubs around Baltimore and in Montgomery County. We could not confirm a museum with dollhouses or miniature rooms on display this time, nor a shop with regular walk-in hours (see the tips). For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-27).

Virtual Dollhouse & Miniatures — Manchester

A miniatures business run by Grace Shaw and her husband Jim in Manchester, in the northeast corner of Carroll County, half a mile from Pennsylvania. It started as a website in 1999, began selling online in 2001 and opened a small store in 2009. The store is in the owners' home and opens by appointment only: call 410-239-2377 (the site asks for at least 24 hours' notice, and at least several hours if you are already nearby; the store is small, so it asks visitors to leave children at home). It specializes in smaller scales and metal miniatures: one inch, half and quarter scale, plus 1:144 and model railroad scales (HO, N and S), with building supplies, lighting, kits and finished pieces. The metal miniatures are not meant for children under 14. There is no shopping cart: you email your list to VirtualDollhouse@verizon.net or graceshaw@verizon.net with your shipping address and phone number, and the shop replies with an invoice. Payment is in US dollars, by PayPal (which adds a fee) or by check (US only). The shop ships internationally, with the cost based on the weight of the parcel; ask for the shipping cost to Canada before you pay. The website's welcome page was updated in August 2026.

Source: virtualdollhouse.net, About us, store terms (orders, shipping, visits), store directions · checked 2026-11-27

Shows

Frederick Miniature Spectacular — Walkersville

A one-day dollhouse and miniatures show and sale run by a group of miniaturists who call themselves “A few of your friendly mini/dollhouse friends.” Next show: Saturday, September 25, 2027, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., at The Grand Social Hall, 79 West Frederick Street, Walkersville, MD 21793, near Frederick. It has a marketplace of handcrafted miniatures, dollhouse décor, collectibles, tools and supplies from artisans and vendors, plus displays. Three hands-on workshops are planned for the same day; details will be posted in July 2027, and you need a show entry ticket before you can sign up for a workshop. Tickets are sold online and are not on sale yet; they are non-refundable. All ages are welcome, and parking is free. The 2026 show was held on October 3.

Source: organizer's 2027 ticket page, organizer's events page, 2026 ticket page · checked 2026-11-27

Clubs

These four clubs are on NAME's club list, and all are listed as accepting new members; the list gives a contact person for each club.

Maryland Miniatures Unlimited — Baltimore area

A NAME club that meets on the third Saturday of the month, September to June, at 10 a.m., at The Almshouse of the Historical Society of Baltimore County, 9811 Van Buren Lane.

Source: NAME club list (linked from miniatures.org) · checked 2026-11-27

Wee Friends of Annapolis

A NAME club that meets once a month on a Saturday at 10 a.m. in members' homes. NAME's list gives Potomac as the club's location, so ask the contact where meetings are held.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-27

Mini Misfits of Damascus — Damascus

A NAME club in Montgomery County. It usually meets on the third Saturday of the month at 1 p.m. at the First Baptist Church of Damascus.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-27

Mini Wonder Workers — Damascus

A second NAME club in Damascus, with the same contact person as Mini Misfits. It meets in a member's home or on Zoom.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-27

Tips

  • Driving down from Canada? Manchester is right on the Pennsylvania state line, so a visit to Virtual Dollhouse fits well with Pennsylvania's shops and shows; see our Pennsylvania page. Always book your visit first.
  • The Baltimore Museum of Art owns a set of miniature rooms by Eugene Kupjack, but its website shows them as not on view right now.
  • For other 2027 shows in the US and Canada, see our events page.
  • Ordering from the US? Ask for the shipping cost to Canada before you pay, and remember that duty and taxes may apply. Read how to avoid scams first.
