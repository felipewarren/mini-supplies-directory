Dollhouse and miniature supplies in British Columbia: shops, shows and clubs

Last checked: October 30, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or club's own website. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

"Are there any dollhouse stores left besides Michaels?" comes up a lot in BC. One Victoria builder asked for local stores with 1:12 wallpaper and trim, and a reply said there are no miniature stores near Victoria anymore. Here's what we could confirm: one walk-in specialist in North Vancouver, three BC-based online shops, the province's big yearly show, and a club that welcomes guests.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Ross' Miniature Treasure House — North Vancouver

A 4,000-square-foot store at 823 West 1st Street that sells dollhouse miniatures only, in business for over 46 years. Most stock is 1:12, plus 1:24 and 1:48. It carries building supplies, interior decorations, furniture, wiring and lights, and has a woodworking shop at the back that makes its own products and custom pieces. Everything shown on its website is in stock in the store. Because of a family illness, the shop asks you to book your visit in advance by email (subject "Store Visit Booking", with your name, phone number and preferred date and time). Phone: (604) 980-2715.

Source: rosstreasurehouse.com · checked 2026-10-30

M for Miniatures — Vancouver

An online shop for miniatures and DIY dollhouse supplies, from paintings, mirrors and frames to wallpaper, flooring and ceilings. All orders ship from Vancouver, usually within 3 business days. In Canada, orders of $100 or more ship free by Canada Post Tracked, and the site's banner also lists $7.50 shipping on orders over $60. Small envelope orders (up to 4 small items) can go by cheaper Lettermail, but that has no tracking or insurance, and the shop can't cover lost Lettermail. Items are listed as 1:6 or 1:12, and we also saw some 1:10 items, so check the measurements before you buy; returns for scale or size preference aren't accepted.

Source: shipping policy, FAQ, homepage · checked 2026-10-30

miniLAND.ca — British Columbia

A store for handcrafted, artist-made miniatures, open since 2004. It started as a local walk-in shop and became web-only in 2015, so there's no storefront to visit. It sells 1:12 furniture and finishing supplies such as wood trim, moulding and wall tiles, and also has a 1:144 section. Orders ship from British Columbia within 1–3 business days with tracking, and shipping is calculated at checkout. Toll-free: 1-866-995-1226.

Source: about us, shipping policy, contact, finishing supplies · checked 2026-10-30

DIY House — Victoria

A Victoria-based seller of DIY kits, including miniature dollhouses and book nooks. The website shows only a selection; for the full catalogue, the shop asks you to email admin@diyhouseonline.com.

Source: diyhouseonline.com · checked 2026-10-30

Ordering online from elsewhere in Canada

Littleyoyo.ca, an online shop that includes dollhouses, ships free to BC on standard orders over $99 (otherwise $14.99 flat), with delivery to BC in 5–6 business days after the order ships. Extra-large items such as dollhouses may add a $19.99 surcharge. Source: shipping policy · checked 2026-10-30. More Canadian online shops are on the Canada page.

Shows and sales in BC

  • Vancouver Dollhouse Miniature Show & Sale, Burnaby: held every May at 4333 Ledger Avenue. The 42nd show was on May 24, 2026, 10 am–3:30 pm; entry was $10 and free for children under 15. It's scent-free, strollers aren't allowed, and there's no ATM on site. The 2027 date isn't posted yet (vancouverminiatureshow.com, checked 2026-10-30).
  • The same organizers ran a free one-day "Mini Miniature Show" with vendors at the Museum of Vancouver on December 7, 2025 (Museum of Vancouver). We found no 2026 edition announced yet.

More shows across Canada are on the events page.

Clubs

Miniature Club of BC (MCBC) — Burnaby

A non-profit club running since 1977, with 40+ members. It meets on the last Sunday of the month from September to June, 9:30 am–12:30 pm, in Activity Room B at Lougheed Mall, 9855 Austin Road, Burnaby, while the Cameron Rec Centre is rebuilt. Meetings include show-and-tell and workshops. Full membership is $50 a year and covers most workshop materials; an out-of-town, newsletter-only membership is $25. Guests can sit in on a meeting first through the club's contact form. Next dates: November 29, 2026 (Christmas party and gift exchange), then January 31, 2027.

Source: miniatureclubofbc.ca · checked 2026-10-30

Tips before you go

  • Ross' is the only walk-in specialist we could confirm in BC, and visits must be booked by email for now.
  • In Victoria, the only specialist seller we found (DIY House) sells online, so compare free-shipping thresholds before you order.
  • The Burnaby show in May is the easiest way to buy from BC makers in person, with no shipping at all.
