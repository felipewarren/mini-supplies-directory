Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Ontario: shops, shows and clubs

Last checked: October 28, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or club's own website. Hours and prices change, so call or check the site before you drive.

"Are there any miniature shops in Ontario?" is one of the most common beginner questions. One new collector asked for shops that are reliable and not overly expensive, and another builder looked for Ontario sources because Etsy shipping adds up fast on tiny parts. This page lists the dollhouse shops you can visit in Ontario, plus the province's shows and clubs.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

The Little Dollhouse Company — Toronto

A 5,000-square-foot shop and workshop at 862 Millwood Rd., with 85 built dollhouses on display and more than 25,000 items in 1:12 scale or smaller. It makes its own dollhouse kits in Ontario. You can order online and pick up in the shop. If your order doesn't qualify for free shipping, the shop's sample rates for a small 2–6 lb package start at $6.00 within Ontario, compared with $15.00 to the rest of Canada.

The shop also runs a one-day electricity workshop on wiring a dollhouse; the next one is November 28, 2026.

Source: homepage, shipping and returns, workshops, contact · checked 2026-10-28

Grandpa's Doll House — St. Thomas

A store at 43624 Sparta Line, in a building behind the house, with thousands of miniatures (many more than are on its website) and some pieces 3D-printed in the shop, such as a 1:12 antique cash register for $9.99. Products are 1:12 unless noted. It also sells lights and wiring supplies and runs workshops. It isn't shipping to the US or the UK right now.

Source: homepage, store hours, shipping and returns · checked 2026-10-28

Georgian Bay Miniatures — Meaford

A 1:12 specialist at 32 Nelson St. E. For DIY builders it carries doors, windows, flooring, trim, wallpaper, shingles, siding and lighting. Its owner, Sue, also refurbishes dollhouses and takes commissions. The shop asks you to call first if you're driving from a distance. The website doesn't mention online ordering or shipping.

Source: georgianbayminiatures.com · checked 2026-10-28

Ordering online from Ontario

Littleyoyo.ca, an online shop that includes dollhouses, offers free standard shipping to Ontario and Québec on orders over $79, compared with $99 for the rest of Canada (otherwise $14.99 flat). Source: shipping policy · checked 2026-10-28. More Canadian online shops are on the Canada page.

Clubs

Miniature Enthusiasts of Toronto (MET)

Founded in 1976. Meets on Monday evenings, usually twice a month, from 7:00 to 9:00 pm at Memorial Community Hall, North York City Centre, 5110 Yonge Street. Membership is $50 a year ($30 for students); members must be 18 or older and are asked to volunteer about 5 hours at the annual show. Workshop supply fees rarely go over $15. The next workshop is on November 16, 2026.

Source: membership, meetings and workshops · checked 2026-10-28

OMEGA (Ontario Miniature Enthusiasts of Guelph Area)

Meets in person on the 2nd Wednesday of the month, 1–3:30 pm, at the Puslinch Community Centre, 23 Brock Road South, and on Zoom on the 4th Wednesday. No meetings in July and August. New members are welcome.

Source: omegaminimakers.weebly.com · checked 2026-10-28

Tips before you go

  • Two of these shops open only a few days a week, so check the hours and call before a long drive.
  • Living near Toronto? Ordering online and picking up at The Little Dollhouse Company skips shipping entirely.
  • Shows are the cheapest way to buy from Ontario makers in person, with no shipping at all.
