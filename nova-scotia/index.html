Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Nova Scotia: shops, suppliers and events

Last checked: November 3, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or event's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

Nova Scotia has one dedicated miniature shop that we could confirm: Freedom Miniatures in Kentville, which sells 1:12 furniture, accessories and building supplies online and in store. For basswood, glue, paint and tools, there are two hobby shops in the Halifax area. Add a Truro miniaturist who sells her work on Etsy and a week-long miniature retreat held in Debert each September. Maritime Hobbies & Crafts in downtown Halifax has closed, so don't plan a trip there.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Annapolis Valley

Freedom Miniatures — Kentville

It calls itself "one of very few miniature stores in all of Canada." The shop sells dollhouse furniture, accessories, outdoor pieces, miniature food, wallpaper, lighting, hardware and dollhouse kits, plus builder supplies like basswood stock, Evergreen polystyrene, landscaping materials and Vallejo paints and pigments. It also has 1:24 and 1:48 sections and sells finished work by its artist-in-residence, Shelley Acker. When we checked, 1:12 furniture included an oak kitchen chair at $11.99, a round oak kitchen table at $20.99 and a brass coat rack at $10.49, and the "Thrifty Thursday" sale section had pieces like a walnut writing desk marked down from $22.99 to $18. In a November 3, 2026 Facebook post, the shop said it had stocked up on Rolife kits, including book nooks. Orders ship by Canada Post Expedited Parcel at a flat $16 anywhere in Canada, and free pickup at the store is offered. The shop has suspended sales to the United States because of the new US duty rules. It's at 45 Webster Street, open Tuesday–Saturday 10 am–5 pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Phone: (902) 698-3673. Email: contact@freedomminiatures.com.

Halifax and Dartmouth

Great Hobbies — Dartmouth

A hobby chain with seven stores in Canada, mostly for radio-control and scale models, not dollhouses. Its online store has a materials section with basswood, birch dowels and plywood, plus adhesives, paints and tools, which are useful for scratch-building. Stock at the Halifax-area store may differ from the online warehouse, so call to check. The store is at 169 Hector Gate, Dartmouth, open Monday–Friday 10 am–9 pm, Saturday 9:30 am–9 pm and Sunday noon–5 pm. Phone: (902) 332-0888. Email: halifax@greathobbies.com.

Source: greathobbies.com, locations and hours · checked 2026-11-03

Nautilus Hobbies — Halifax

A Halifax hobby shop (it shares a business with an aquarium store) with plastic models, model railroad supplies, die-cast, model rockets, and a tools, paints and supplies section with paints, hobby tools, airbrushes and decals. It isn't a dollhouse store, but it's a central place for paint and tools. It's at 6442 Quinpool Road, open Monday–Saturday 10 am–5 pm. Phone: (902) 454-4296.

Source: nautilushobbies.ca, tools, paints and supplies · checked 2026-11-03

Makers

Nasus Miniatures — Truro

Susan Mattinson, a Truro miniaturist who competed on the TV show "Best in Miniature," has made miniatures since 2018 and specializes in threadcraft such as knitting, micro crochet and embroidery. She's been a member of the International Guild of Miniature Artisans (IGMA) since 2018. She sells through an Etsy shop and also offers patterns, video tutorials and a free yarn label PDF. If you live in or near Truro, she says you can message her to arrange delivery or pickup. We couldn't load the Etsy shop itself, so check prices and shipping there.

Events

Camp Mini Ha Ha — Debert

An annual miniaturist retreat held since 2001, open to all levels, with campers welcome from anywhere. The 2026 camp ran September 8–13 at the Debert Hospitality Centre, 130 Ventura Drive, with one group project ("Fractured Fairy Tales," led by Shelley Acker) that could be built in any scale. The 2026 basic fee was $1,100, covering five nights' accommodation, meals, the project and workshops, and sales tables were $20 a day. The 2027 dates and fee aren't posted yet. Registration is by a form you email in. Contact: aleetaj@hotmail.com.

Source: campminihaha.com, contact, FAQ, 2026 registration form · checked 2026-11-03

Tips

  • Freedom Miniatures is the only Nova Scotia shop we found with a full 1:12 range. For more choice, compare the online shops on the Canada page.
  • Rolife and other book nook kits aren't sold as a standard 1:12 scale, so check the scale before buying them for a dollhouse.
  • Old directories still list Maritime Hobbies & Crafts in Halifax. The company filed for bankruptcy in September 2024, and its old .com address now shows an unrelated website.
