Dollhouse and miniature shops and shows in Washington State

Last checked: November 13, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or show's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Washington is the closest US state for many British Columbia builders, and it has a walk-in dollhouse shop north of Seattle and one of the oldest miniature shows in North America. This page lists what we could confirm. For online US shops, see the USA page; for BC shops, see the BC page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-13).

Miniatures & More — Edmonds

A dollhouse miniature store in Edmonds with an online shop. It sells furniture, 1:12 kits, building supplies (trim, glue, paints), garden pieces and gift cards, and it offers small-group classes for 2 to 4 people. Address: 610 5th Avenue South, Suite D, Edmonds, WA 98020 ("under Off Main and across from Ace Hardware"). Open Monday to Saturday, 11 to 5:30; closed Sunday. Phone: 425-835-0541. Email: info@miniaturesmore.com. Shipping terms, including to Canada, aren't stated on its homepage, so ask before ordering.

Source: miniaturesmore.com · checked 2026-11-13

Country Store Miniatures — Vancouver, WA

Calls itself "your complete miniature shop" for dollhouses and miniature displays, and says it will try to find what you need if it doesn't have it. It lists a P.O. box (P.O. Box 61881, Vancouver, WA 98666), not a storefront, plus an eBay store called Price Right Miniatures. Phone: 360-695-1425. It sells at the Seattle show, where its table carries building supplies, furniture and accessories in 1", 1/2" and 1/4" scale.

Source: countrystoreminiatures.com; 2026 Seattle dealer list · checked 2026-11-13

Maple & Moss Miniatures — Tacoma

Not a supply shop: Maple & Moss finds, researches and restores vintage and heirloom dollhouses and places them with new owners. It also restores family dollhouses. Phone: 253-332-1624. Email: info@mapleandmossminiatures.com.

Source: mapleandmossminiatures.com · checked 2026-11-13

Shows

Seattle Miniature Show (PNW Miniature Shows)

Running since 1978, this is one of the region's biggest dollhouse miniature shows, with dealers, exhibits and workshops. The 2027 dates are March 5–7, 2027, with a new preview show on Friday, March 5, 6 to 9 p.m., and Saturday, March 6, 9 to 10 a.m. The organizers say contracts and tickets will be "available very soon", so the general hours, prices and venue for 2027 aren't posted yet. The 2026 show was at the DoubleTree Suites by Hilton Southcenter (16500 Southcenter Parkway, in Tukwila, just south of Seattle).

The same organizers run the Portland Miniature Show in Oregon, next held in October 2027.

Source: pnwminiatureshows.com, show history · checked 2026-11-13
