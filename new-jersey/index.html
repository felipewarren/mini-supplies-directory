Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and museums in New Jersey

Last checked: November 19, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, show's, club's or museum's own website (for the library club, the library's own calendar). Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

New Jersey has two full-service dollhouse shops in the north of the state, the big Philadelphia-area show in Cherry Hill, a model-railroad attraction in Flemington with a doll museum and a 94-room dollhouse, and two miniature clubs. Clinton and Flemington are both in Hunterdon County, so the shop and the museum fit in one day. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-19).

Karen's Dollhouse Shop — Clinton

A family-run dollhouse shop in business since 1990, in a 1,400-square-foot store on Main Street in historic Clinton since May 2021. It carries dollhouses, room boxes and kits; furniture and accessories for every room; dolls; half-scale, quarter-scale and 1:144 items; building supplies (flooring, shingles, doors, windows, stairs, trims); lighting and electrical parts; and wallpaper, along with work by many artisans and estate collections. It also restores dollhouses (painting, wallpaper, flooring, wiring and lighting) and sells at shows around the country. Address: 14 Main Street, Clinton, NJ 08809. Phone: 908-766-3777. The website lists Monday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Sunday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., and says the store is open seven days a week until the New Year; call before a long drive. Its shipping page covers US orders and says international orders are charged the actual shipping cost, which the shop tells you before it ships; it does not name Canada.

Source: karens-dollhouse-shop.myshopify.com, about page, shipping and returns · checked 2026-11-19

Miniature Dollhouse Creations — West Milford

A full-service dollhouse shop that has moved to a larger space on the upper level of 1616 Union Valley Road, across from the pet store. It sells dollhouses, custom room boxes, furniture for every room, porcelain, resin and hand-sculpted artisan dolls, lighting, flooring, building and landscaping supplies, miniature food and accessories, with brands such as Reutter Porcelain, Houseworks, Town Square Miniatures, Real Good Toys, Itsy Bitsy and MiniGraphics wallpaper, Cir-Kit, Sir Thomas Thumb and Bespaq. It offers video tours and takes miniature collections and dollhouses on consignment. Address: 1616 H Union Valley Rd., West Milford, NJ 07480. Phone: 973-800-8808. Posted hours: Monday to Friday 11:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., Sunday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. The website is a simple page with no online shop or dates, so call to confirm before you go.

Source: miniaturedollhousecreations.com · checked 2026-11-19

Shows

Philadelphia Miniaturia — Cherry Hill

The big November show for the Philadelphia area is held in Cherry Hill, New Jersey, near Philadelphia (it is also on our Pennsylvania page). Next show: November 5 to 7, 2027. Public show and sale: Saturday, November 6, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Sunday, November 7, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.; general admission is paid at the door, or you can buy a ticket online ahead of time. Opening night and weekend passes go on sale on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Location: DoubleTree by Hilton Philadelphia/Cherry Hill, 2349 W. Marlton Pike, Cherry Hill, NJ 08002. Free parking.

Source: philadelphiaminiaturia.com · checked 2026-11-19

Museums

Northlandz — Flemington

A huge indoor miniature attraction, best known for its model railroad (8 miles of track, a Guinness world record in 1997). It also has a doll museum with more than 200 dolls, including antique, vintage and celebrity dolls, and a 94-room dollhouse, plus an art gallery. Address: 495 US-202, Flemington, NJ 08822. Phone: 908-782-4022. Its website lists it as open seven days a week, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.; the outdoor train ride runs only on weekends and holidays. Call before you go.

Source: northlandz.com, hours and location, doll museum page · checked 2026-11-20

Clubs

Jersey Inchkins Club — Red Bank

Members build 1-inch-to-1-foot miniature houses, rooms, scenes and vignettes together on the third Thursday of every month, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m., at the Red Bank Public Library, 84 W. Front Street, Red Bank, NJ 07701 (library phone: 732-842-0690). The next session on the library's calendar is Thursday, December 17, 2026.

Source: Red Bank Public Library calendar, library contact page · checked 2026-11-19

NJ Miniatures Group — Warren County

A free group for people who make, collect and share dollhouse miniatures, meeting in western New Jersey. Members work in 1", 1/2", 1/4" and 1/144 scale, and beginners are welcome. The group tries to meet monthly, but how often depends on interest and the time of year. Most members are from northern New Jersey, but anyone is welcome, and there is no cost to join. Use the contact form on its website to ask about the next meetup.

Source: njminiatures.com, contact page and FAQ · checked 2026-11-19
