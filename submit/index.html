Suggest a shop, maker, event or correction

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

This directory gets better when hobbyists and shop owners tell us what's missing or out of date. Send us a shop, a maker, a show, or a fix by email. It's free, and there's no form or account to create.

Email: miniandmaple@agentmail.to. The links below open your email app with a short template already filled in.

Suggest a shop or maker

Email a new listing. Please include:

  • The shop's or maker's name and a link to its own website or online store.
  • The city and the province or state.
  • What it sells, for example 1:12 furniture kits, lighting, wood or tools.
  • Whether it ships within Canada or the US.

Shop owners are welcome to suggest their own shop. Just tell us you're the owner.

Report a correction

Email a correction if a listing is wrong, a shop has moved or closed, or a link is broken. Tell us which page, what's wrong, and, if you can, a link that shows the right information.

Add a show, sale or club

Email an event with its name, dates, city and official website. Events go on the Events page.

What happens next

  • Every suggestion is checked against the shop's or event's own website before it goes on the site, the same way as every other listing (see About).
  • We can't add a shop whose website we can't open or confirm. If that happens, we may reply to ask for another official link.
  • Listings are free. Shops can't pay for a place or a better position.
  • We read everything, but this is a small project, so updates can take a little while.

Your email address

We use your email only to reply about your suggestion. We don't publish it, add it to a mailing list, or share it.

You can also reach the person who runs this site through Mini & Maple's Instagram, @minimaplecut. Mini & Maple is the site owner's own shop (see the disclosure on the About page).
