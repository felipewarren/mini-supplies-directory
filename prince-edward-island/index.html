Dollhouse and miniature supplies on Prince Edward Island: shops and suppliers

Last checked: November 5, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

We couldn't confirm a dedicated dollhouse shop on Prince Edward Island. What the Island does have is the headquarters store of a national hobby chain in Stratford (next to Charlottetown) with styrene, basswood, paints and tools useful for scratch-building, a comics and games store in downtown Charlottetown that carries miniature paints, and an independent art-supply shop for adhesives, knives and acrylics. We didn't find a PEI miniature show, club or museum listing we could confirm from its own source. For a full 1:12 range, the nearest dedicated miniature shop we know of is Freedom Miniatures in Kentville, Nova Scotia (see the Nova Scotia page); Elm City Hobbies near Fredericton also ships to PEI (see the New Brunswick page).

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Stratford / Charlottetown area

Great Hobbies — Stratford

A Canadian hobby chain founded in 1984, with its head office and PEI store at 17 Glen Stewart Drive in Stratford, just outside Charlottetown. It focuses mainly on radio-control and scale models, not dollhouses, but its catalog includes Evergreen Scale Models styrene sheet and strip, Model Expo basswood sheet, adhesives, paints and tools that are useful for scratch-building. When we checked, the Stratford store showed stock of clear Evergreen styrene sheet (EVG9006 at $12.99); a Model Expo basswood sheet SKU was listed online but showed zero stock at Stratford that day, so call or check the product page before a special trip. Online orders ship from a Mississauga warehouse by Canada Post Expedited (usually 4 to 7 business days to most centres). For a limited time, non-oversize parcels of $99 or more in product get free basic shipping within Canada (insurance still applies; oversize and over-length fees may apply). It is not shipping to U.S. addresses because of duty and tax rule changes. The PEI store is open Monday–Friday 10 am–6 pm and Saturday 10 am–5 pm, closed Sunday. Phone: (902) 569-3262. Email: pei@greathobbies.com.

Source: about, locations and hours, shipping, Evergreen styrene example, basswood example · checked 2026-11-05

The Comic Hunter — Charlottetown

A comics, trading-card and games store with a Charlottetown location at 183 Queen Street. It isn't a dollhouse supplier. Its online catalog includes an Army Painter paints and supplies section (when we checked, listed Army Painter Speedpaint markers among other items), which is useful if you paint small figures; stock labels on the site often show which of its Maritime stores holds an item, so confirm Charlottetown stock before you go. The site lists Charlottetown hours as "Please Inquire," so call (902) 566-2926. It also has stores in Moncton and Fredericton, New Brunswick.

Source: comichunter.net, supplies catalog, FAQs and store information · checked 2026-11-05

Blank Canvas Art Supplies — Charlottetown

An independent art-supply shop in Confederation Court Mall (125 Great George Street, 1st floor, behind the escalators). It isn't a miniature or dollhouse store: the focus is fine-art paints, brushes, paper, canvas and related supplies. For miniaturists it's a walk-in source of acrylic paints, adhesives, knives and brushes, and it offers special-order requests and free local pickup on online orders. Posted hours for September–December are Monday–Saturday 10 am–6 pm and Sunday noon–4 pm; check the site for other seasons.

Source: blankcanvasartsupplies.ca, about · checked 2026-11-05

Tips

  • We couldn't confirm a dedicated 1:12 dollhouse shop, club or miniature show on PEI. The Free Miniaturist Directory's Canada page has an empty Prince Edward Island section. For furniture, kits and building supplies, compare the online shops on the Canada page.
  • Freedom Miniatures in Kentville, Nova Scotia is the closest dedicated miniature shop we found (see the Nova Scotia page). Elm City Hobbies near Fredericton lists flat-rate shipping to PEI (see the New Brunswick page).
  • Hobby and games shops mostly sell kits and figures in smaller scales, not 1:12 dollhouse scale. Check the scale before buying figures or accessories for a dollhouse.
