Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and museums in Illinois

Last checked: November 16, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, show's, club's or museum's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Chicago is one of the big names in the miniature world. Every April it hosts the Chicago International show, and the same week a second show runs in Schaumburg. The city also has two famous 1:12 collections on public view: the Thorne Miniature Rooms at the Art Institute and Colleen Moore's Fairy Castle at the Griffin Museum of Science and Industry. This page lists what we could confirm, including a hobby shop with a dollhouse department in Lemont, a doll and dollhouse shop in Chicago and a local club. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-16).

Oakridge Toys, Hobbies & Gifts — Lemont

A family-owned business that began in 1987 as a hobby kit maker and mail-order retailer of scale models, model railroading and dollhouse miniatures. Today its store in the Old Mill Shoppes in downtown Lemont combines a hobby shop, toy and gift shop, dollhouse shop and resale shop, with dollhouses, kits and miniatures. Oakridge also makes its own scale doors, windows and trims, from 1:87 to 1:12, including "shallow wall" parts for half-inch and 1-inch Greenleaf dollhouses. Address: 15800 New Ave., Lemont, IL 60439. Hours: Monday to Thursday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., Friday 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Sunday 12 to 5 p.m. (holiday hours may differ). Phone: 630-435-5900. The store carries only a sample of the online catalog, so call before you go for a specific item. The online shop says it ships worldwide and has many regular customers in Canada; its free-shipping offers are for the lower 48 states only.

Source: oakridgestores.com showroom page, stores page, shipping and returns · checked 2026-11-16

Gigi's Dolls & Sherry's Teddy Bears — Chicago

A family-owned doll and teddy bear shop in Chicago's Norwood Park neighbourhood, open since 1981. Along with antique, vintage and modern dolls and bears, it sells dollhouse miniatures, doll furniture, accessories and clothing, and offers doll repair and appraisals. Address: 6029 N. Northwest Hwy, Chicago, IL 60631. The shop is open Tuesday to Saturday by appointment only; call or email first. Phone: 773-594-1540. Email: questions@gigisdolls.com. It also sells through its online shop and a large eBay store. International orders ship by USPS Priority Mail, and international customers pay any taxes or duties.

Source: gigisdolls.com, FAQ · checked 2026-11-16

Shows

Chicago International Miniature Show (Tom Bishop) — Chicago

The organizer calls it the largest gathering of miniaturists in the world, with dealers from 20 countries and 35 US states. Next show: Saturday, April 10, 2027, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Sunday, April 11, 2027, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. A ticketed preview show and sale runs Friday, April 9, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Saturday, April 10, 9 to 10 a.m.; preview tickets must be bought ahead and are not sold at the door. Workshops with miniature artists run from Monday, April 5. Location: Marriott Chicago O'Hare, 8535 W. Higgins Road, Chicago, IL 60631. Admission is paid by cash, cheque or Zelle only; credit cards are not accepted.

Museums

Thorne Miniature Rooms, Art Institute of Chicago

In the 1930s Narcissa Niblack Thorne gathered Chicago artisans to build a series of period rooms at 1:12 scale, a visual history of interior design. Thorne and her craftsmen made 68 rooms, and the museum calls them much-loved. They are in Gallery 11 on the lower level. Address: 111 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago. Hours: Monday, Wednesday and Friday to Sunday 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., Thursday 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., closed Tuesday, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day.

Source: artic.edu Thorne Rooms page, visit page · checked 2026-11-16

Colleen Moore's Fairy Castle, Griffin Museum of Science and Industry — Chicago

Silent film star Colleen Moore had this 1:12-scale castle built from 1928 to 1935. It holds more than 1,500 miniatures in rooms such as the Great Hall, library, chapel, dining room, kitchen and Magic Garden, and it came to the museum in 1949, where it has its permanent home. It is shown behind glass, with a narrated story. Address: 5700 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive, Chicago, IL 60637. Check the museum's site for the day's hours.

Source: griffinmsi.org exhibit page, the story · checked 2026-11-16

Clubs

Wee 'c' Miniature Club — Oakbrook Terrace

A N.A.M.E. club for the Chicago area that meets in Oakbrook Terrace on the fourth Friday of the month. Its site shows its yearly club projects (the 2026 project is a scene built in a book box), exhibit nights and holiday parties. To join, email the club through the address on its website.
