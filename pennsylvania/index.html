Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and museums in Pennsylvania

Last checked: November 17, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, show's, club's or museum's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Pennsylvania has a long-running dollhouse shop in Pittsburgh, a 1:12-scale shop inside a market in Morgantown, two one-day shows in Lancaster and the Lehigh Valley, and, until June 2027, a big exhibition of antique dollhouses in Bethlehem. Pittsburgh also has a famous miniature village. The well-known Philadelphia show is listed here too, but note that it is held across the river in Cherry Hill, New Jersey. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-17).

Lynlott Miniatures (Dollhouse Junction) — Pittsburgh

A dollhouse and miniatures shop with more than 40 years of experience. Its Pittsburgh store is filled with thousands of products from the major manufacturers, select artisans, estate collections and custom-built dollhouses, and it also sells wallpapers, battery-powered lights and building components. The shop builds and restores dollhouses and can help with assembly, wiring and miniature design. Address: 223 Commercial Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15215, in Aspinwall, across the river from the Pittsburgh Zoo. Hours: Monday to Thursday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Friday and Saturday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., closed Sunday and on major holidays; evenings by appointment. Phone: 412-781-6445. If an item is not in stock when you visit, the shop says it will ship it to you for free. Its online shop ships to Canada at a flat international rate (see its shipping page); free shipping is for the continental US only, and international customers pay any taxes and import duties.

Source: dollhousejunction.com about page, contact and hours, shipping info · checked 2026-11-17

Mazie's Miniatures — Morgantown

A shop of "real estate and home decor in 1:12 scale", located inside Morgantown Market. Address: Morgantown Market, 2940 Main St., Morgantown, PA 19543. Open daily 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Phone: 610-913-1953. The market's own site lists holiday changes (for 2026: closing at 1:30 p.m. on November 25, closed on Thanksgiving, November 26, and open 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Black Friday, November 27) and says to check its Facebook or Instagram for bad-weather closings. Mazie's was also a dealer at the 2026 Lehigh Valley show below.

Source: maziesminiatures.com, location page, Morgantown Market contact page · checked 2026-11-17

Shows

Lancaster Miniature Dollhouse Show — Lancaster

A one-day show and sale. Next date: Sunday, May 16, 2027, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Location: Farm and Home Foundation of Lancaster County, 1383 Arcadia Road, Lancaster, PA 17601. The 2026 show had more than 20 dealers. There is a free "Leave it and Take it" table: bring miniatures you no longer need, or take something you've been looking for.

Source: dollhouseminiatureshow.com · checked 2026-11-17

Lehigh Valley Dollhouse Show & Sale — Easton

The 48th annual show and sale of the Lehigh Valley Small on Scale club (see Clubs below). Next date: Sunday, August 22, 2027, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Location: Charles Chrin Community Center, 4100 Green Pond Road, Easton, PA 18045. Free parking; no strollers. Show proceeds support local charities.

Source: lehighvalleyminiatures.com show page · checked 2026-11-17

Philadelphia Miniaturia — Cherry Hill, New Jersey (Philadelphia area)

The big November show for the Philadelphia area, held just across the state line in New Jersey. Next show: November 5 to 7, 2027. Public show and sale: Saturday, November 6, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Sunday, November 7, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.; general admission is paid at the door, or you can buy a ticket online ahead of time. Opening night and weekend passes go on sale on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Location: DoubleTree by Hilton Philadelphia/Cherry Hill, 2349 W. Marlton Pike, Cherry Hill, NJ 08002. Free parking. (Also on our New Jersey page.)

Source: philadelphiaminiaturia.com · checked 2026-11-17

Museums

Art of the Dollhouse, Kemerer Museum of Decorative Arts — Bethlehem

A temporary exhibition of nearly 60 antique dollhouses and structures, some as tall as a child, and more than 6,000 miniatures, on view until June 27, 2027. Many come from the Elizabeth Johnston Prime collection, bequeathed to Historic Bethlehem Museums & Sites in 2006, with houses by makers such as Christian Hacker and Moritz Gottschalk. There are also modern dollhouses and scenes such as a train station, stables and small storefronts. Hours: Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., with more days and hours during the holiday season. Self-guided and docent-guided tours and "Collectors' Hour" sessions are booked on the museum's website. Address: 427 N. New St., Bethlehem, PA 18018.

Source: historicbethlehem.org exhibition announcement · checked 2026-11-17

Miniature Railroad & Village, Kamin Science Center — Pittsburgh

Not a dollhouse display, but a classic for anyone who loves miniatures: a model village of Western Pennsylvania landmarks, with the largest train display in the region and hundreds of animated scenes of life up to 1940. Recent additions include a model of the Andrew Bayne Memorial Library. It is on the second floor and included with general admission. Address: One Allegheny Ave., Pittsburgh, PA 15212. Phone: 412-237-3400. Check the science centre's hours page before you go; it lists closed days and holiday closures.

Source: kaminsciencecenter.org exhibit page, hours and closures · checked 2026-11-17

Clubs

Lehigh Valley Small on Scale — Lehigh Valley

A N.A.M.E. club (chapter C285) founded in 1978, which runs the Lehigh Valley show above. It meets on the first Tuesday of every month except June (summer picnic) and December (holiday party): social time from 6:30 p.m., then a business meeting at 7 p.m. followed by a small project. Members also get together for monthly daytime "play dates". To join, use the contact addresses on the club's website.

Source: lehighvalleyminiatures.com about page · checked 2026-11-17

Tips

  • Call before a long drive. Lynlott is closed on Sundays.
  • The Lancaster and Lehigh Valley shows are one-day Sunday events. A trip from Canada to just one show may be shorter than 24 hours, which means no personal exemption.
  • The Bethlehem dollhouse exhibition is temporary: it ends June 27, 2027.
  • Ordering from the US? Ask for the shipping cost to Canada before you pay, and remember that duty and taxes may apply. Read how to avoid scams first.
