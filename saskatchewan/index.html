Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Saskatchewan: shops, suppliers and clubs

Last checked: November 2, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or club's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

Saskatchewan has no dedicated dollhouse shop that we could confirm. What we did find: a Regina hobby room with DIY miniature house kits, a Regina collector shop with vintage doll accessories, Saskatoon's big hobby store for scratch-building supplies, a small Western Canada mail-order business that sells miniature findings and silk ribbon, and the Saskatoon miniature club. If you want a club that meets online, the Edmonton club welcomes members from across Canada.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Regina

Zippity Zoom Hobby Room — Regina

The hobby side of Zippity Zoom Toys, a family-owned Regina toy store open since 2009. The Hobby Room opened in November 2023 at 4623 Albert St, a few steps from the toy store at 4601 Albert St. Its "DIY Miniatures" section has book nooks and miniature house kits, mostly Rolife when we checked (for example, a Christmas wreath kit at $54.99, a Sakura Journey 3D puzzle set at $44.99 and a display box at $34.99; the Snow House kit was sold out). Rolife kits aren't sold as a standard 1:12 scale, so check the scale if you're furnishing a dollhouse. The Hobby Room also stocks paints and inks (such as AK Interactive), abrasives, airbrushes and casting, molding and glue supplies. Open Tuesday–Saturday 10 am–5 pm, closed Sunday and Monday. The website offers in-store pickup, free local delivery on orders over $60 and shipping in Canada. Phone: 306-522-4622. Email: hobby@zippityzoom.ca.

Source: zippityzoomtoys.ca, Hobby Room, DIY Miniatures, about · checked 2026-11-02

Bills Diecast and Hobby Room — Regina

Mostly a diecast car shop, but its online store had 17 results for "doll" when we checked, mainly vintage doll accessories: a set of vintage pantry pieces in pottery, metal and plastic that the shop calls "perfect for the doll house" ($60), vintage bathroom accessories ($35) and a small wooden bookshelf ($5). These are one-off secondhand pieces and the scale isn't stated, so ask for measurements first. The shop lists its address as 1825A Park Street, but its home page also says it is physically located inside the Funky Gifter at 1230 Broad St., so call before you go. Listed hours are Monday–Saturday 10 am–6 pm and Sunday 11 am–5 pm. Online items can be shipped, delivered locally or picked up. Phone: (306) 450-9167.

Source: bills-diecast-and-hobby-room.square.site, pantry accessories page, "doll" search · checked 2026-11-02

Saskatoon

Express Hobbies — Saskatoon

It calls itself Saskatchewan's largest hobby shop, with over 4,500 square feet of retail space. It isn't a dollhouse store, but it stocks what builders use: wooden ship kits, glue, building materials, paint, airbrushes, tools, hardware and scratch-building material. It's at 411 34th Street East, in the Diamond Center strip mall at 34th and Quebec, near downtown. Open Monday–Friday 10 am–5:30 pm and Saturday 10 am–5 pm, closed Sunday. It takes mail orders from Canada and the USA by phone or email, and online orders are paid by e-Transfer. Phone: 306-954-3434. Email: info@expresshobbies.com.

Source: expresshobbies.com, about, how to find us · checked 2026-11-02

Mail order from Western Canada

Another World Miniatures

A small cottage business, started in 2009 and based in Western Canada, that has brought dollhouse miniature supplies to shows in Western Canada; its owners say they belong to the Saskatoon Miniature Society. The site lists findings and accessories (for example, a brass door knob with key at $3, a pair of brass hinges with nails at $4 and a tiny metal birdcage at $4), Made in Canada hand-woven baskets, quick kits, "Mini-Stash Builder" packs of small bits like wood, lace and trim, and silk ribbon in over 70 shades in 2 mm and 4 mm widths. There's no shopping cart: you send an order inquiry through the contact form with your mailing address, and they email the total with shipping and a PayPal invoice. Prices are in Canadian dollars, and it ships to Canada and the continental United States.

Check first: the products page says items are still being added, and the contact page still mentions COVID-19, so the quantities shown may be out of date. Ask before you count on an item.

Source: anotherworldminiatures.ca, about, products, contact · checked 2026-11-02

Clubs

Saskatoon Miniature Society

Saskatoon's miniature club. Its Facebook page describes it as "Sharing our passion for all things miniature. Located in Saskatoon, Canada." Meeting times and fees aren't shown publicly, so message the page to ask about joining.

Source: Saskatoon Miniature Society on Facebook · checked 2026-11-02

Online option: Miniature Enthusiasts of Edmonton

Not in Saskatchewan, but this Edmonton club meets on Zoom and says its 90-plus members come from across Canada. It meets online every Tuesday at 7 pm MST: a general meeting on the first Tuesday, a workshop on the third, and chats on the other Tuesdays. Membership is $20 a year (kits extra). Email meemembership@gmail.com to join.

Source: miniature-enthusiasts-of-edmonton.com · checked 2026-11-02

Tips

  • No Saskatchewan shop we found carries a full 1:12 range, so for dollhouse furniture and building supplies, compare the online shops on the Canada page and the Alberta and Manitoba pages.
  • Vintage doll accessories and kits sold for display often aren't 1:12. Ask for measurements and use the scale check.
  • Bills Diecast and Another World Miniatures sell one-off or small-quantity items, so call or email before you count on one.
