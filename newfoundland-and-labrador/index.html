Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Newfoundland and Labrador: shops and suppliers

Last checked: November 6, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

We couldn't confirm a dedicated dollhouse shop in Newfoundland and Labrador. What the province does have is a long-running hobby shop in St. John's with basswood, Vallejo paints, plastic cement and model-building tools useful for scratch-building, a Mount Pearl games store with a large paints-and-accessories section (plus Warhammer tools), and an art-supply shop in Mount Pearl for acrylics, brushes and related studio materials. We didn't find an NL miniature show, club or museum listing we could confirm from its own source. For a full 1:12 range, the nearest dedicated miniature shop we know of is Freedom Miniatures in Kentville, Nova Scotia (see the Nova Scotia page); Elm City Hobbies near Fredericton lists flat-rate shipping to Newfoundland and Labrador (see the New Brunswick page).

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

St. John's / Mount Pearl area

Signal Hobbies — St. John's

A St. John's hobby shop at 36 Pearson Street (postal code A1A 3R1 on its site). It focuses on radio-control, plastic model kits and model trains, not dollhouses, but its catalog is useful for scratch-builders: when we checked, a basswood search returned 24 results (including strip and block sizes, with examples such as 1/32 × 1/4 × 24 basswood strips at $0.79 and a 2 × 4 × 12 basswood block at $17.99), the Vallejo brand listing showed 328 results (washes, primers, putty and varnishes among them), and a plastic-cement search listed Testors and other styrene cements. The brand filters on the site also include Evergreen Scale Models, Midwest, Plastruct and Woodland Scenics; call to confirm current sheet or strip stock before a special trip. The site lists curbside pickup Monday–Saturday (closed Sundays and statutory holidays) and phone-in hours 9 am–9 pm daily; confirm walk-in hours when you call. Phone: (709) 722-7021. Email: signalhobbies@bellaliant.com.

Source: signalhobbies.com, basswood search, Vallejo listing, plastic cement search · checked 2026-11-06

Midgard Gaming — Mount Pearl

A games store at 7 Commonwealth Avenue in Mount Pearl. It isn't a dollhouse supplier. Its online catalog includes an RPGs & Minis → Paints & Accessories section (when we checked, 101 paint items and 69 brushes-and-accessories items) and a Warhammer section with miniatures, Citadel products and tools, which is useful if you paint small figures. Posted hours are Monday–Friday 10 am–10 pm, Saturday 10 am–10 pm and Sunday noon–6 pm. Phone: (709) 745-5052. Email: store@midgardgaming.com.

Source: about, paints and accessories, Warhammer · checked 2026-11-06

Art Cansel — Mount Pearl

An art-supply shop at 1133 Topsail Road in Mount Pearl (A1N 5G5). It isn't a miniature or dollhouse store: the focus is painting, drawing, drafting and framing supplies, plus framing and related services. For miniaturists it's a walk-in source of acrylic paints, brushes and studio materials; the site lets you browse inventory, with purchasing on the site described as coming soon. Posted hours are weekdays 7:30 am–4:30 pm, Saturday 10 am–4 pm and Sunday closed. Phone: (709) 738-3600. Email: info@artcansel.com. (An older art-supplies page still mentions 210 Kenmount Road for pickup; the contact page and site footer both list Topsail Road, so use that address.)

Source: artcansel.ca, contact and hours, shop art supplies · checked 2026-11-06

Tips

  • We couldn't confirm a dedicated 1:12 dollhouse shop, club or miniature show in Newfoundland and Labrador. The Free Miniaturist Directory's Canada page has an empty Newfoundland and Labrador section. For furniture, kits and building supplies, compare the online shops on the Canada page.
  • Freedom Miniatures in Kentville, Nova Scotia is the closest dedicated miniature shop we found (see the Nova Scotia page). Elm City Hobbies near Fredericton lists flat-rate shipping to Newfoundland and Labrador (see the New Brunswick page).
  • Hobby and games shops mostly sell kits and figures in smaller scales, not 1:12 dollhouse scale. Check the scale before buying figures or accessories for a dollhouse.
