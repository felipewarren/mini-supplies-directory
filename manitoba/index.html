Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Manitoba: shops and shows

Last checked: November 1, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or show's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

The strongest walk-in option we found for 1:12 pieces is a long-running Winnipeg toy store with a large dollhouse miniatures section. Around it, we could confirm a hobby store for paints and tools, a small toy and craft shop, and a doll and miniature show that runs in Winnipeg. Everything we found is in Winnipeg; we couldn't confirm a miniature source elsewhere in the province.

Winnipeg stores

Toad Hall Toys — Winnipeg

Open since 1977 and family run, it calls itself Manitoba's largest and oldest independent toy retailer. Its dollhouse page has a "1/12 Scale Dollhouse" section with 352 items listed when we checked, such as Christmas tree kits from Creative Little Details, wreaths, stockings, mini food like Christmas cake and brownies, and a candy tin from International Miniatures (for example, a Christmas tree kit at $24.99 and a wreath at $2.99), with in-store stock shown for each item. It also carries toy dollhouses and furniture (Haba, Hape, Le Toy Van) and Rolife DIY miniature house kits. These toy lines aren't all 1:12, so check the scale. The store is at 54 Arthur Street, open Monday–Saturday 10 am–5 pm and closed Sunday (on November 11 it opens 1–5 pm). It offers curbside pickup and local delivery within the Perimeter for $15; shipped orders go the cheapest way available, with the cost added to your order, and it doesn't ship to the USA. Phone: 204-956-2195. Email: customer_service@toadhalltoys.ca.

Source: about, dollhouses, 1/12 scale list, hours, ordering and shipping · checked 2026-11-01

Hobby Sense — Winnipeg

A hobby shop and online store at 34-845 Dakota St, not a dollhouse specialist. It sells plastic and wooden model kits, paints, tools, weathering supplies and display materials, plus puzzles and embroidery kits. Shipping is free on orders of $120 and up (before taxes), within Canada only. From November to April, water-based paints, primers, cements and varnishes can freeze in transit, and the store says it isn't responsible for frozen paint. Open Tuesday–Thursday 11 am–5 pm, Friday 11 am–6 pm and Saturday 10 am–4 pm; closed Sunday and Monday. Phone: (204) 254-1165.

Source: hobbysense.ca, about, shipping policy · checked 2026-11-01

Aunt Monica's Attic — Winnipeg

An independent toy, puzzle and craft shop in Transcona at 111A Regent Ave W. For miniaturists it has Sam & Julia laser-cut wooden dollhouse furniture kits made for the Mouse Mansion toy house ($19.99 each, paint not included), plus craft supplies such as adhesives, felt, paint and wood craft. Its website shows store pickup as the way to get online orders. The shop jokes about its hours, roughly 10 am to 6 or 7 pm Tuesday–Saturday and noon–6 pm on Sunday, with Monday listed as "sometimes", so call or check before a special trip.

Source: auntmonicasattic.com, Sam & Julia kit page · checked 2026-11-01

Shows and sales in Manitoba

  • Winnipeg Doll Extravaganza, Winnipeg: a show and sale of handmade, vintage and collectible dolls, teddy bears, toys and dollhouse miniatures, including one-of-a-kind pieces by local artists. In 2026 it ran twice at the Viscount Gort Hotel, 1670 Portage Avenue: Sunday, April 12, 10 am–4 pm (admission $5 for adults, $3 for children under 12), and Sunday, October 25, 10 am–4 pm ($5 admission). The next date isn't posted yet; watch the organizer's Facebook page or email underthemushroomminis@gmail.com (organizer's Facebook page, April 2026 listing, October 2026 Winnipeg Free Press notice, checked 2026-11-01).

Tips

  • Toad Hall's 1/12 section is the closest thing to a dollhouse shop in Manitoba. Item pages show in-store stock, so check the page before going downtown.
  • Ordering paints or cements online between November and April? Water-based products can freeze in transit, so pick them up in store or order before the cold.
  • For 1:12 building supplies and furniture you can't find locally, compare the online shops on the Canada page and their free-shipping thresholds.
