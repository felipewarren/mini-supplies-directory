Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Alberta: shops, makers, shows and clubs

Last checked: October 31, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or club's own website. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

A Calgary parent asked where to find 1:12 pieces beyond Michaels, and a local miniaturist replied that Calgary doesn't have many places to buy mini pieces. We couldn't confirm a walk-in dollhouse specialist anywhere in Alberta. Here's what we could confirm: two Alberta makers who sell online, two Calgary stores with related stock, and an Edmonton club with a yearly show and online meetings anyone in Canada can join.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Alberta makers who sell online

MiniMinutia — Edmonton

Run by Tina MacDonald, a miniaturist for more than 45 years and the workshop coordinator of the Edmonton club listed below. The shop has sold on Etsy since 2016 and calls itself the "Home Depot" for quarter-scalers: wallpaper (including brick, stone and kids' patterns), gingerbread trim, shingles, wooden flooring, doors and windows, and laser-cut kits in 1:12, 1:24 and 1:48. It also sells handmade baskets and wheel-thrown pottery. Her website has free videos on building book nooks. Email: tina@camacdonald.com.

Source: miniminutia.com (links to its Etsy shop) · checked 2026-10-31

Fantastic Minis — Alberta

A one-person studio (Kristy Wipf) making handcrafted dollhouses, furniture, trees, mini food and other supplies, with no 3D printing. It also takes custom commissions and quote requests. There's no storefront: it offers online support and private appointments by request, and you ask for delivery details and pricing on custom work. Phone: 780-554-4046 (Monday–Friday, 8 am–5 pm Mountain time). Email: kwipf47@gmail.com.

Source: fantasticminis.ca, contact, miniature supplies · checked 2026-10-31

Calgary stores with related stock

PM Hobbycraft — Calgary

A big general hobby store (models, RC, tabletop games, paints, tools) at 2020J 32 Ave NE, not a dollhouse specialist. It carries diorama building supplies, hobby paints and glues, and wood craft kits including Rolife book nooks and miniatures. A Calgary miniaturist on Reddit said it has lots of diorama material but few individual mini pieces. Shipping is a flat $23 anywhere in Canada, free on orders over $250, and online orders can be picked up free in store with photo ID. Its homepage and shipping page list different Sunday hours (9:30 am–6 pm versus 11 am–5 pm), so call (403) 291-2733 before a Sunday trip.

Source: pmhobbycraft.ca, shipping, pickup and returns · checked 2026-10-31

Castle Toys — Calgary

A toy store at 101 - 5718 1A Street SW with "Dollhouses" and "Dollhouse Furniture" categories; its brands include Calico Critters and Robotime. These are toys, so check the scale before buying for a 1:12 house. Open Monday–Saturday 10 am–6 pm and Sunday 11 am–5 pm. Phone: 403-258-1100.

Source: castletoys.ca · checked 2026-10-31

Shows and sales in Alberta

  • MEE Dollhouse Show & Sale, Edmonton: the Miniature Enthusiasts of Edmonton's yearly show. The 2026 show was on Saturday, September 19, 10 am–4 pm, at Central Lion's Seniors Rec Center, 11113 113 Street, with 25+ vendors plus displays; admission was $8 for adults, $3 for ages 7–15, and free under 7. The 2027 date isn't posted yet; the club lists meeshowandsales@gmail.com for the 2027 show (MEE show page, checked 2026-10-31).

More shows across Canada are on the events page.

Clubs

Miniature Enthusiasts of Edmonton (MEE)

A club running since January 1982, with more than 90 members from across Canada (and a few from the US). It meets online, so you can join from anywhere: every Tuesday at 7 pm Mountain time on Zoom. The first Tuesday is the general meeting with show-and-tell, the third Tuesday is a workshop of about two hours, and the other Tuesdays are open chats. It also holds one or two all-day Saturday workshops a year. Membership is $20 a year and covers meetings, workshops and chats (kits cost extra). Beginners are welcome; contact meemembership@gmail.com.

Source: miniature-enthusiasts-of-edmonton.com · checked 2026-10-31

Tips

  • We found no walk-in dollhouse specialist in Alberta, so for 1:12 furniture and building supplies, compare the online shops on the Canada page and their free-shipping thresholds.
  • The Edmonton show in September is the easiest way to buy from Alberta makers in person, with no shipping at all.
  • For landscaping and scenery, PM Hobbycraft's diorama supplies are a local option in Calgary.
