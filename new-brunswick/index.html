Dollhouse and miniature supplies in New Brunswick: shops, suppliers and events

Last checked: November 4, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's or event's own page. Hours and prices change, so check the site or call before you go.

We couldn't confirm a dedicated dollhouse shop in New Brunswick. What the province does have is a well-stocked model shop near Fredericton that ships across Canada, two hobby and game stores in Moncton and Saint John for paints and tools, a yearly miniature and doll show in Riverview each spring, and a handmade family dollhouse you can see at a museum in Hampton. For a full 1:12 range, the nearest dedicated miniature shop we know of is in Kentville, Nova Scotia (see the Nova Scotia page).

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec shop, which is not listed on this page. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Fredericton area

Elm City Hobbies — Hanwell

A model shop just outside Fredericton, mostly for plastic kits, model railroad and rocketry, but with a scratch-building section (Evergreen styrene, K&S and Albion Alloys metal tubing and rod, Smooth-On), a diorama section, Vallejo and other paints, and tools and finishing supplies. It isn't a dollhouse store and we didn't see basswood listed, but it's handy for styrene, metal stock, paint and tools. If something is out of stock, the owner says he can usually special-order it. Shipping is a flat rate to any Canadian address: $17 to New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and PEI, $19 to Ontario and Québec, $23 to the Prairies, and $24 to BC, the territories and Newfoundland and Labrador. Orders with aerosol cans go by Canada Post. For card payments online it needs a real credit card, not a debit Visa or Mastercard, and it also accepts Interac e-Transfer. The store is at 1779 Route 640 (Hanwell Road), open Tuesday–Thursday and Saturday 10 am–6 pm and Friday 10 am–9 pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Email: scott@elmcityhobbies.com.

Moncton

Board Miniatures Hobby Store — Moncton

A hobby store and gaming space whose owner started out painting miniatures. It's aimed at tabletop gaming (Games Workshop), LEGO, trading cards and scale models, not dollhouses, but its paints, terrain and weathering section and its glue, tools and accessories section are useful for any miniature project. When we checked, it listed AK weathering pencils at $2.99, a 12 ml pot of Leadbelcher paint at $5.99 and packs of static grass tufts at $12.99. It's at 105-70 Granite Drive, open Tuesday 5:30–9 pm, Wednesday–Thursday noon–9 pm, Friday noon–10 pm, Saturday 10 am–10 pm and Sunday 10 am–8 pm, closed Monday. Phone: (506) 383-6464. Email: info@boardminiatures.com.

Source: boardminiatures.ca, about, contact and hours · checked 2026-11-04

Saint John

Heroes' Beacon — Saint John

A comics and games store with a gaming lounge. It runs a "Let's Paint! Miniatures Paint Night" where you can bring your own supplies or use the store's community paints and learn from other painters; check its calendar for the next date. It isn't a dollhouse supplier, but it's a place to practise painting small figures and meet other hobbyists. It's at 25 Charlotte Street, open Monday–Wednesday 10 am–9 pm, Thursday–Friday 10 am–10 pm, Saturday 10 am–9 pm and Sunday 10 am–4 pm. Phone: (506) 214-5755. Email: heroes@heroesbeacon.com.

Source: heroesbeacon.com, paint night · checked 2026-11-04

Events

Moncton Miniature & Doll Show & Sale — Riverview

A one-day show and sale with vendors from four provinces selling miniatures, dolls, dollhouses, doll clothing, and handcrafted and commercial miniatures. The 3rd annual show was on Saturday, May 2, 2026, 9 am–3 pm, at the Dan Bohan Centre, 5 Fatima Drive, Riverview. It's wheelchair accessible with free parking, and admission was $4 for adults and free for children under 10. The 2027 date isn't posted yet. The listing is on the Town of Riverview's events page; the town doesn't organize it.

Source: Town of Riverview event listing · checked 2026-11-04

Worth a visit

Kings County Museum — Hampton

A local history museum that, according to CBC News, received the Clover Hill dollhouse in 2024: a hand-built replica of the Cassidy family farmhouse in Norton, made by Allan Cassidy in his Fredericton workshop, with details like a miniature Starr stove, a working rolltop desk and a handmade quilt. The museum's own site doesn't mention the dollhouse, so call ahead to confirm it's on display. The museum's posted off-season hours are Thursday–Saturday 1–4 pm, and admission is by donation. Phone: (506) 943-0424. Email: kingscountymuseumnb@gmail.com.

Source: kingscountymuseum.com, CBC News, November 16, 2024 · checked 2026-11-04

Tips

  • For 1:12 furniture, kits and building supplies, compare the online shops on the Canada page. Freedom Miniatures in Kentville, Nova Scotia is the closest dedicated miniature shop we found (see the Nova Scotia page).
  • Older directories list the Moncton Miniature and Doll Club, but the club dissolved in 2023 after 42 years. The Riverview show listed above is held under the Moncton Miniature & Doll Show name.
  • Gaming and model shops mostly sell kits and figures in smaller scales, such as the 1:35 military kits at Elm City Hobbies, not 1:12 dollhouse scale. Check the scale before buying figures or accessories for a dollhouse.
