Dollhouse and miniature shows, museums, clubs and sellers in Texas

Last checked: November 22, 2026. Each listing was checked against the show's, museum's, club's or seller's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Texas is big, and its miniature scene is spread out. The main event is the Texas Miniature Showcase in Dallas each June, a juried show with workshops, dealers and club exhibits. Two museums show dollhouses: a dollhouse gallery at a contemporary art museum in Plano, north of Dallas, and a collection of miniature houses at the windmill museum in Lubbock. We also list a statewide miniature society and a Lubbock club, plus three Texas makers who sell online: Texas-themed 1:12 miniatures from Dallas, wood dollhouse kits for 1:6 dolls from the Hill Country, and artisan miniature ceramics from Kyle. We could not confirm a walk-in dollhouse shop in Texas on its own website. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-22).

Texas Tiny — Dallas

An online shop for unique Texas-themed miniatures in 1:12 scale. It sells cowboy and western pieces (cowhide chairs and rugs, saddles, hats, mounted longhorns), Hispanic and Mexican miniatures (Talavera-style tile fireplaces and tables, piñatas, retablos, Day of the Dead pieces), American Indian pieces, plated Tex-Mex food and Texas produce crates, and Texas garden pieces, along with miniatures from other makers. Orders ship by USPS within three days of payment; international buyers can add insurance only with USPS Priority Mail. Phone: 214-341-7341 (Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central time). The website lists no shop to visit.

Source: texastiny.com, shipping page, contact page · checked 2026-11-22

Martin Dollhouses — Johnson City

A Texas maker that has designed and made wood dollhouse kits since 1990. The kits are sized for 1:6 scale (playscale) fashion dolls, not 1:12 dollhouse miniatures. Each kit has 10 to 15 numbered pieces cut from 3/8-inch birch plywood, takes about 2 hours to assemble, and comes with nails, glue and a square. The Premium kit is the basic house; the Deluxe kit adds windows, shutters, doors, siding and shingles. It also sells an "Action Figure City" kit and 1:6 scale accessories. Phone: 830-225-4435. Sold online; there is no shop to visit.

Source: martindollhouses.com, contact page · checked 2026-11-22

Creations by Lynette — Kyle

An IGMA Artisan who makes fine miniature ceramics by hand: wheel-thrown or hand-built, glazed and kiln-fired bowls, vases, urns and teapots, plus a few other pieces. Each piece is one of a kind and signed, and the photo shows the piece you receive. Scales: 1:12, 1:24, 1:48 and 1:144. Sold through the online shop on the website; orders placed in December ship after January 1. Creations by Lynette was a dealer at the 2026 Texas Miniature Showcase.

Source: creationsbylynette.net, Texas Miniature Showcase dealer list · checked 2026-11-22

Shows

Texas Miniature Showcase — Dallas

A juried dollhouse miniature show with dealers, workshops and exhibits from clubs, including the Dallas Miniature Guild, Downsizers, Lil' Bits Miniature Club, Lonestar Miniatures, Miniatures Under Glass Sisterhood (MUGS), N.U.T.S., NAME and the Society of American Miniaturists. Next show: June 3 to 6, 2027. Friday, June 4: preview evening, 6 p.m. to 9:30 p.m., for preview pass holders. Saturday, June 5: doors open at 10 a.m. (preview ticket holders get in at 9 a.m.). Saturday auctions benefit charity. The 2027 workshops go on sale on Black Friday, November 27, 2026. Location: DoubleTree by Hilton near the Galleria, 4099 Valley View Lane, Dallas, TX 75244; parking is free for show visitors. Contact: info@texasminiatureshowcase.us.

Source: texasminiatureshowcase.us, tickets and workshops page · checked 2026-11-22

Museums

Dollhouse Hall at MOCVA — Plano

The Museum of Contemporary Visual Art (MOCVA) in Plano, in the northern Dallas–Fort Worth area, has a Dollhouse Hall among its permanent galleries. The dollhouses range from Victorian-era designs to modern styles, with detailed rooms, tiny chandeliers, wallpaper and silverware. Address: 1201 E. 15th Street, Suite 304, Plano, TX. Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Admission is paid; members get in free.

Source: Dollhouse Hall page, visit page · checked 2026-11-22

American Windmill Museum — Lubbock

Best known for its huge collection of restored windmills, this museum also shows the miniature houses of local artist Alta Reeds. In the newer wing, several of her houses are part of the early-1900s scenery of the large model train layout, and the rest of the collection is under glass in the southwest corner of the wing. The museum's Christmas Villages display runs from November 17, 2026, to January 2, 2027. Address: 1701 Canyon Lake Drive, Lubbock, TX 79403. Phone: 806-747-8734. Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday. Admission is paid.

Source: Windmill Experience page, admission page, windmill.com · checked 2026-11-22

Clubs

Society of American Miniaturists (S.A.M.) — statewide

A nonprofit miniature society founded by Dallas miniaturists in 1982. It is based in Texas and has more than 200 members, most of them in Texas. It meets twice a year: the Birthday Party, a one-day workshop event in the spring whose location rotates around the state, and the Wonderful Workshop Weekend, held in Killeen in September. Its home page also promotes the Texas Miniature Showcase. You can join through its website.

Source: minisam.org, history page, upcoming events · checked 2026-11-22

Miniaturists of Lubbock — Lubbock

A club of miniature enthusiasts who share ideas and sources and build miniatures together. Members work in all scales, but 1:12 is the most popular. Meetings: the fourth Saturday of the month at 2 p.m. at Covenant Presbyterian Church, 4600 48th Street, Lubbock. All ages and skill levels are welcome; children under 18 must come with an adult. Each member keeps a basic tool kit (the list is on the club's site), and each year the club picks a theme for a room or house to show at its annual fall show and sale at the church. The club's site still shows the 2024 show, so check its social media links for the next dates.

Source: miniaturistsoflubbock.carrd.co · checked 2026-11-22
