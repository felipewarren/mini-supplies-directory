Dollhouse and miniature shops, museums, shows and clubs in Florida

Last checked: November 26, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, museum's, show's or club's own website (for clubs, the club list of the National Association of Miniature Enthusiasts, NAME). Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Florida is a winter favourite for Canadians, and it has a lot for miniaturists, though we could not confirm a walk-in dollhouse shop on its own website this time. The Lakeland Miniature Guild holds its 42nd annual show and sale in Lakeland on May 23, 2027, with a preview, a Fun Day and workshops the days before. In winter, the Miniature Art Society of Florida shows hundreds of tiny works of fine art in Dunedin, near Tampa, and in Sarasota, The Ringling's Circus Museum is home to the Howard Bros. Circus Model. We also list two Florida sellers that sell online and at shows, and six NAME clubs around the state. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-26).

Miniature Designs — Pensacola

A dollhouse miniature business that has been around for more than 36 years. In 2026 it closed its walk-in store in Braselton, Georgia, and moved to Pensacola, Florida, where it is not open to walk-in visitors: it now sells only on its website and at about four shows a year. It says it carries more than 15,000 miniatures, including dollhouse kits, room boxes, furniture, lighting and accessories in 1:12, 1:24 and 1:48 scale, lines such as Houseworks, Real Good Toys, Bespaq, Aztec Imports and Town Square Miniatures, and estate pieces. Phone: 770-923-8500. Email: dollhouses@miniaturedesigns.com. The shop ships outside the United States by USPS international mail (allow up to 4 weeks; duty and taxes are extra), but its free shipping offer on orders over US$85 does not apply to Canada. The website warns that postage may not calculate properly at checkout; if that happens, it asks you to choose "in store pickup" and it will email you the shipping cost before shipping. Its events page lists the Atlanta and Chicago shows in spring 2027.

Source: store page, shipping and returns page, upcoming events · checked 2026-11-26

Genie's Mini House Shop — Port St. Lucie

Handmade 1:12 and 1:6 scale miniatures made since 2020 by Genie Rosado in Port St. Lucie. She specializes in nurseries and children's rooms: cribs, bassinets, room boxes and furniture, along with small kitchen and holiday pieces. The online shop lists about 160 items, priced in US dollars. Phone: 772-530-4850. Email: geniesminihouseshop@yahoo.com. The shop also sells at shows (it was a vendor at the 2026 Atlanta show); its website says new show dates will be posted once the schedule is set. The website doesn't say whether it ships to Canada, so ask before you order.

Source: geniesminihouseshop.com, contact information, Atlanta show vendor list · checked 2026-11-26

Shows and exhibitions

Lakeland Miniature Guild Dollhouse Miniature Show & Sale — Lakeland

The 42nd annual show and sale of the Lakeland Miniature Guild, a NAME club. Next show: Sunday, May 23, 2027, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., at the SpringHill Suites by Marriott, 511 W. Lime Street, Lakeland, FL 33805. A preview with early buying runs on Saturday, May 22, from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. (the preview ticket also covers Sunday), followed by an optional dinner buffet that must be paid by May 5, 2027. Admission is paid, with a lower price for children 6 to 12; tickets are sold at the admissions table, and parking is free. The show has a silent auction, displays of club projects, a sales table for children only and a free children's workshop. The same weekend, the guild holds pre-registered workshops on Friday, May 21 (details not posted yet), and its Fun Day on Saturday, May 22 (8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., project: a French Country Kitchen; registration form on the website).

Source: 2027 show page, Fun Day page, Friday workshops page · checked 2026-11-26

Miniature Art Society of Florida, 52nd International Miniature Art Exhibition — Dunedin

Fine art in miniature rather than dollhouse pieces: more than 400 tiny works from around the world plus over 100 from the society's permanent collection, including oil painting, scrimshaw, drawing and sculpture. During the exhibition, artists demonstrate how they work, often under magnification. The society's website and its 2027 prospectus give the dates as January 17 to February 14, 2027, but its event page says the exhibition runs until February 21, so confirm before going. A Collectors Preview (no invitation needed) is on Saturday, January 16, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., and the public opening on Sunday, January 17, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. (the event page shows a 2 p.m. start on January 17, so confirm). Place: Dunedin Fine Art Center, 1143 Michigan Boulevard, Dunedin, FL 34698, near Tampa. Phone: 727-298-3322. The art center is open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., with free parking.

Source: MASF annual exhibition page, 2027 prospectus (PDF), MASF event page, Dunedin Fine Art Center visit page · checked 2026-11-26

Museums

Circus Museum at The Ringling (Howard Bros. Circus Model) — Sarasota

The Ringling's Circus Museum opened in 1948 as the first museum of its kind and fills two buildings, the Historic Circus Galleries and the Tibbals Learning Center. For miniature fans, the highlight is the Howard Bros. Circus Model, a model circus in the Tibbals Learning Center, which the museum suggests you be sure to see. Entry to the Circus Museum is included with Museum Admission, which also covers the Museum of Art and the Bayfront Gardens. Address: 5401 Bay Shore Road, Sarasota, FL 34243. Hours: daily, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. (on Thursdays only the Museum of Art and the gardens stay open after 5 p.m.); closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day, and closes at 5 p.m. on Christmas Eve. Admission is paid (children under 6 and members free), and adults and seniors save by buying online. Parking is free. Because of road construction on US 41, the museum suggests reaching Bay Shore Road via College Drive from the north, or via Myrtle Street, 47th Street or Mecca Drive from the south.

Source: Circus Museum page, visitor information, tickets and admission · checked 2026-11-26

Clubs

These six clubs are on NAME's club list. Five are listed as accepting new members; the list gives a contact person for each club.

D.I.M.E. — Deltona

A NAME club in the Daytona Beach area. It meets on the first Saturday of every month, all year, from noon to 4 p.m., at the Deltona Regional Library, 2150 Eustace Avenue, Deltona, FL 32725.

Source: NAME club list (linked from miniatures.org) · checked 2026-11-26

Les Petits Collecteurs of South Florida — Boca Raton

A NAME club in Palm Beach County. It meets on the first Wednesday of the month, 7 p.m. to 9 p.m., at the Whisper Walk Clubhouse in Boca Raton.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-26

Miniature Memories — Davie

A NAME club in Broward County, near Fort Lauderdale. It meets on the first Tuesday of the month at 7 p.m. at the Bamford Community Center, 3801 S. Pine Island Road, Davie, FL 33328.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-26

Mini-de-Lights of Ft Myer — Cape Coral

A NAME club in the Fort Myers area, on the southwest coast. It meets on the second Saturday of the month at 10:30 a.m. at Gulf Coast Village in Cape Coral.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-26

Brevard Association of Miniatures — Palm Bay

A NAME club on the Space Coast that meets in members' homes; the club's contact on NAME's list gives meeting details.

Source: NAME club list · checked 2026-11-26

Lakeland Miniature Guild — Lakeland

The club behind the Lakeland show, active for more than 40 years and a past winner of NAME's Club of the Year award. Members build a different club project each year, and monthly meetings include workshops on new techniques. It meets on the fourth Monday of the month at 5:30 p.m. at United Presbyterian Church, 178 Lake Hollingsworth Drive, Lakeland. NAME's list shows the guild as not accepting new members, while the guild's own website has a new-member contact, so ask before you plan to join.

Source: guild's About page, NAME club list · checked 2026-11-26

Tips

  • Spending part of the winter in Florida? The miniature art exhibition in Dunedin runs from mid-January to mid-February 2027, and The Ringling in Sarasota is open every day except a few holidays.
  • Many Florida clubs meet all year. Contact one through NAME's club list before a visit to ask if guests are welcome.
  • For other 2027 shows in the US and Canada, see our events page.
  • Ordering from the US? Ask for the shipping cost to Canada before you pay, and remember that duty and taxes may apply. Read how to avoid scams first.
