Dollhouse and miniature supplies in Québec: shops, shows and clubs

Last checked: October 29, 2026. Each listing was checked against its own website unless it's marked Unverified. Hours and prices change, so check before you go.

Dollhouse builders in Québec often find that, apart from the chain craft stores, there's little that's dollhouse-specific nearby. One Québec City collector asked exactly that and mostly got pointed to wargaming and hobby shops. Here's what we could actually confirm: the province's big yearly show, two clubs, a few shops, and online stores that ship to Québec for free above a set amount.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Québec City shop, which is listed below and clearly labelled. For shops elsewhere in Canada, see the Canada page.

Shops

Mini & Maple — Québec City (site owner's shop)

Handmade, laser-cut 1:12 supplies for miniature artists, for example the Mini 1:12 Cutting Board & Paddle Set (CAD $9). The shop also sells personalized engraved maple gifts. It's online only, with no pickup, and ships within Canada only: $15 flat, free over $75.

Source: miniandmaple.square.site · checked 2026-10-22 (the 1:12 range and the $9 set appear on the site; shipping terms confirmed by the owner)

Boutique LeoLudo — Laval and Montréal

A toy store, not a collector's shop. Its dollhouse section carries wooden children's dollhouses, furniture sets and doll families, mostly from Le Toy Van and Tender Leaf Toys, for example a Dollhouse Starter Furniture Set at $109.95. It's a good fit for a child's first dollhouse, but these aren't labelled 1:12 collector pieces.

The store is in CF Carrefour Laval, 3003 boul. le Carrefour (Mon–Wed 10 am–7 pm, Thu–Fri 10 am–9 pm, Sat 9 am–5 pm, Sun 10 am–5 pm). Online orders ship free in Québec and Ontario over $100 (before taxes) and cost $10 below that. Elsewhere in Canada it's $15, or free over $150. You can also pick up for free at the Montréal warehouse, 9250 avenue du Parc, unit 222, Mon–Fri 10 am–5 pm.

Source: dollhouses, shipping policy, contact · checked 2026-10-29

Les Ateliers MenuArt — Saint-Hyacinthe

Unverified: the shop's website, menuartminiatures.com, did not load when we checked on 2026-10-29. A search-engine copy of it says the website is permanently closed and that products will be sold only at shows.

Business directories still list a dollhouse and miniatures shop at 2335 av. Sainte-Anne, run by miniaturist France Cabana, who holds IGMA artisan status. Since the shop may have closed, call 450-250-6368 before you go.

Source: menuartminiatures.com · last load attempt 2026-10-29 (failed); directory listing on Yellow Pages

Online shops with free shipping to Québec

  • Littleyoyo.ca, an online shop that includes dollhouses: free standard shipping to Québec and Ontario over $79, compared with $99 for the rest of Canada (otherwise $14.99 flat). Delivery to Québec takes 2–3 business days to most urban areas after the order ships. Source: shipping policy · checked 2026-10-29.
  • The Little Dollhouse Company (Toronto): free shipping in Canada over $100, but dollhouses and kits are excluded. See the Ontario page for details.
  • More Canadian online shops are on the Canada page.

Shows and exhibitions

  • MEM Dollhouse Miniature Exhibition and Sale, Montréal area: April 3–4, 2027. Entry is $10 and free for children 12 and under. Entry is cash only, though some dealers take cards. The club's directions point to a hotel off Highway 40 at the Morgan exit (mem-miniature.com, checked 2026-10-29). The 2026 show was at the Courtyard by Marriott, 20000 Trans-Canada Hwy, Baie-D'Urfé, according to the club's Facebook post.
  • Coati-Minis at the Circuit des Arts Coaticook, Eastern Townships: the miniature artists' group showed its work at the Station B – Bibliothèque Françoise-Maurice on July 18–19, 2026, and also exhibits at Coatic'Art's biennial show at the Musée Beaulne. 2027 dates aren't posted yet (coaticart.com, checked 2026-10-29).

Clubs

Miniature Enthusiasts of Montreal (MEM)

Artisans of all levels who meet every month for a workshop to make a miniature item. Membership is $45 and includes the monthly kits. Everyone is welcome, but workshops are in English. The club runs the yearly show above.

Source: about us · checked 2026-10-29

Coati-Minis — Coaticook

A French-speaking group of miniature artists within Coatic'Art, the Coaticook-area visual artists' association, founded in April 2013. Members make miniature houses and objects, dolls, furniture, tiny paintings and sculptures. Anyone with the passion and patience for miniature work can join; members also belong to Coatic'Art. Contact: Coatic'Art, 34 rue Main E., Coaticook.

Source: coaticart.com/coati-minis · checked 2026-10-29

Tips for Québec buyers

  • Search in French too. Many Québec sellers use "maison de poupée", "miniatures" and "échelle 1:12" rather than the English terms.
  • The MEM show is the easiest way to buy from many makers at once without paying shipping; bring cash for the entry.
  • For a child's first dollhouse, a toy store like LeoLudo is fine. For 1:12 collector pieces, use the shows and the shops on the Canada page.
