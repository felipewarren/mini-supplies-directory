Dollhouse and miniature museum, shows and shops in Colorado

Last checked: November 21, 2026. Each listing was checked against the museum's, show's or seller's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

Colorado's miniature scene centres on the Denver Museum of Miniatures, Dolls & Toys in Lakewood, just west of Denver. The museum shows large dollhouses and hundreds of artisan miniatures, runs a gift shop with scale miniatures, hosts free and low-cost meetups for miniaturists, and organizes two miniature shows: a one-day show in March and a big two-day show in September. We also list a miniatures and kits booth in Castle Rock and a family of miniature makers in Centennial who sell on Etsy. We found no full-line dollhouse shop in Colorado with a working website. For online US shops, see the USA page; for more shows, see the events page.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-21).

Denver Museum of Miniatures, Dolls & Toys gift shop — Lakewood

The museum's gift shop sells quality scale miniatures, locally made artisan miniatures, dolls, paper dolls, plush and historic toys, and books. It is open to the public and you don't need a museum ticket to shop. The museum also has an Etsy shop and mails the pieces it posts there. Address: 830 Kipling Street, Lakewood, CO 80215. Phone: 303-322-1053. It keeps the museum's hours: Friday and Saturday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Sunday 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.

Source: gift shop page, dmmdt.org · checked 2026-11-21

Tiny Small Worlds — Castle Rock

A miniatures booth inside The Emporium, a market of more than 60 local businesses in historic downtown Castle Rock. It sells handcrafted miniatures, models and miniature kits (such as Rolife DIY mini house kits), fairy garden supplies, art and fantasy handbags. It leans toward kits and fairy gardens rather than dollhouse building supplies. Address: The Emporium, 200 Perry Street, Castle Rock, CO 80104. The Emporium is open daily, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., except some holidays.

Source: Tiny Small Worlds page, theemporiumcastlerock.com · checked 2026-11-21

David's Dollhouse — Centennial

A family business making handcrafted miniatures since 1978. David makes laser-cut kits in 1-inch and 1/4-inch scale and handmade furniture, toys and games; other family members and a friend make one-of-a-kind holiday decorations, Christmas trees, ornaments and wreaths, and spooky or cute handmade miniatures. Handmade one-of-a-kind pieces are sold in the David's Dollhouse Etsy shop, and manufactured and vintage miniatures in the Dreamhouse Miniatures Etsy shop. Phone: 303-550-8528. There is no shop to visit. David's Dollhouse was a dealer at the museum's 2026 Fall Show.

Source: davidsdollhouse.com, 2026 Fall Show dealer list · checked 2026-11-21

Shows

The 'little' Show — Lakewood

The museum's one-day miniature show, now in its 35th year. Next show: Saturday, March 13, 2027, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Location: Green Mountain Presbyterian Church, 12900 W. Alameda Parkway, Lakewood, CO. Admission is paid; advance tickets are sold online.

Source: the 'little' show page · checked 2026-11-21

Fall Miniatures Show — Lakewood

The Denver area's annual dollhouse miniatures show, run in support of the museum, with five days of workshops and two days of public sales rooms. Next show: September 11 to 12, 2027 (theme: "Changing Seasons"). Sales room hours: Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Admission to the sales room is paid, and the museum recommends buying it ahead of time online. No strollers. Location: Sheraton Denver West, 360 Union Boulevard, Lakewood, CO 80228.

Source: Fall Show page · checked 2026-11-21

Museums

Denver Museum of Miniatures, Dolls & Toys — Lakewood

A museum founded in 1981 and devoted to miniatures, dolls and toys, now in its own building in Lakewood. Current exhibits include the largest dollhouses in its collection, the Someday House and the Santa Fe House, two new dollhouses by Mariana Cameron, Bill Lankford's "Somewhere in Time" town, antique dollhouses, room boxes made by friends of the museum, more than 300 artisan miniatures, and dolls, bears and toys from the collection. A new elevator now makes the building accessible. Address: 830 Kipling Street, Lakewood, CO 80215; the entrance is just east of the traffic light at Kipling Street and 8th Place (the museum warns that GPS apps may send you to 8th Avenue instead). Phone: 303-322-1053. Hours: Friday and Saturday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Sunday 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.; last entry at 3 p.m. Closed on major holidays, including Easter, the Fourth of July, Thanksgiving and Christmas, and on New Year's Day 2027.

Source: dmmdt.org, exhibits page, events calendar · checked 2026-11-21

Clubs

Miniaturist meetups at the museum — Lakewood

The Denver Museum of Miniatures, Dolls & Toys hosts three regular gatherings for miniaturists at 830 Kipling Street. Mini Meetup is free, every second Saturday from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m.: bring your project and your questions (next dates: December 12, 2026, and January 9, 2027). Small Talk is an informal gathering for all skill levels on the fourth Saturday of the month, 1 p.m. to 2:30 p.m., with low-cost kits and basic supplies; gallery admission is not included, and the museum asks you to RSVP. Inch x Inch is a small mini club for newcomers that meets on the first Saturday of the month at 10 a.m.; space is limited, so contact the museum through the form on its page. Check the events calendar before you go, since holiday workshops can change the schedule.

Source: events calendar, Small Talk page, Inch x Inch page · checked 2026-11-21
