Dollhouse and miniature shops, shows and museums in New York State

Last checked: November 15, 2026. Each listing was checked against the shop's, show's or museum's own website. Hours and dates change, so call or check before you go.

New York State borders both Quebec and Ontario, so it's an easy trip for many Canadian builders. The state has a long-running dollhouse shop in Manhattan, a full-service dollhouse shop in Syracuse, a Hudson Valley dealer who sells artisan collections online, a December miniature sale in White Plains and a 24-room dollhouse on view in Yonkers. This page lists what we could confirm. For online US shops, see the USA page; for Ontario and Quebec, see the Ontario and Quebec pages.

Disclosure: no listing on this site is paid. The person who runs this site also runs Mini & Maple, a Canadian shop that does not ship to the US, so it isn't listed here.

Bringing purchases home to Canada? Same-day trips get no personal exemption. After more than 24 hours away you can bring back up to CAN$200 in goods duty- and tax-free, and after more than 48 hours up to CAN$800. Declare everything at the border (CBSA guide, checked 2026-11-15).

Tiny Doll House — Manhattan

A dollhouse and miniatures shop on the Upper East Side that calls itself the only dedicated dollhouse and miniatures shop in New York City. It has been open for over 30 years and stocks handmade miniatures from artisans around the world, from one-of-a-kind and limited-edition collector pieces to everyday items for children and beginners: dolls, houses, furniture and accessories. Address: 314 East 78th Street, New York, NY 10075. Hours: Monday to Friday 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., closed Sunday. Phone: 212-744-3719. The site's Shop link goes to its Etsy shop; check shipping to Canada there.

Source: tinydollhouse.com, contact page, about page · checked 2026-11-15

Lee Thomas Miniatures — Syracuse

A full-service dollhouse shop in a Queen Anne Victorian house in Syracuse, serving upstate New York for over 30 years. It sells dollhouses, furniture, interior decor, accessories, electrical fixtures and supplies, finishing materials and books, and offers appraisals, dollhouse renovation and restoration, and custom miniatures. Address: 1816 West Genesee Street, Syracuse, NY 13204. The showroom is open by appointment on Saturdays, Sundays, Mondays and Wednesdays, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Phone or text: 315-484-7215. Email: customerservice@leethomasminiatures.com. Shipping is free within the continental US; international orders ship by the cheapest carrier, and you can ask for the cost by email. Between Thanksgiving and January 1, online orders are taken by request only, through customer service.

Source: leethomasminiatures.com, contact page, store policies, FAQ · checked 2026-11-15

D. Thomas Fine Miniatures — Hastings-on-Hudson (online)

A Hudson Valley business that started as a retail gallery in Hastings-on-Hudson and now works online, selling curated artisan dollhouse miniature collections through estate sales and auctions, with current listings on its eBay selling page. Its newsletter announces upcoming sales, and it also helps collectors and families sell collections. It posts regularly on its blog (latest post November 2, 2026). Check each eBay listing for shipping to Canada.

Source: dthomasfineminiatures.com, about page · checked 2026-11-15

Shows

Westchester Dollhouse Miniature Sale — White Plains

A one-day sale of new, handcrafted and estate miniatures, run by the Dollhouse Revivals restoration studio. Next date: Sunday, December 6, 2026, 10:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., at the Women's Club of White Plains, 305 Ridgeway, White Plains, NY 10605. Guided make-and-take classes for children run at 12 and 2 p.m. for the cost of supplies. Tickets are sold online through the organizer's site.

Source: dollhouserevivals.com, upcoming shows · checked 2026-11-15

Museums

Hudson River Museum — Yonkers

The museum's 24-room dollhouse Nybylwyck Hall, made by Mark O'Banks, is the centerpiece of the exhibition "Collection Spotlight: The Comforts of Home" (on view from October 30, 2026, ongoing). The house spans four floors and holds over 900 interior objects, including staircases, cross-stitched carpets and hand-papered walls. Address: 511 Warburton Avenue, Yonkers, NY 10701. Hours: Wednesday to Friday 12 to 5 p.m., Saturday and Sunday 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Free parking.

Source: hrm.org exhibition page, Nybylwyck Hall page, visit page · checked 2026-11-15
